## Flow States
Flow States are powerful abilities that exist outside the normal action economy. They represent a character operating beyond their usual limits.

### Noesis
Flow States cost **Noesis**, a rare and expensive item that can only be acquired while playing or by the Conductor.

### Choosing Your Flow States
You select one Flow State at **Levels 1, 6, and 12**. Once chosen, a Flow State cannot be changed.

### Using Flow States
Activating a Flow State is a **Free Action**. Only one Flow State may be activated per round. Some Flow States have durations or early-end conditions — these are specified in the ability text.
If a Flow State interacts with an ongoing effect in an ambiguous way, default to the more dramatic interpretation.





## How Life Flows
Manifest a devastating Storm in Very Long Range around you that lasts for 3 rounds. While the storm is active you gain access to the following Free actions:
The Wind: Push a Being up to 10 tiles in any direction. 
The Lightning: Attack a Being for pure 4d6 Entropy damage.
The Hail: Reduce both the Movement and Attack Value of a Being by 15.
All actions can be used but only once per turn each.

## Astral Projection 
Your spirit leaves your physical body. Time stops completely and you may take one action while in this state. Attack a Being for damage equal to four times your highest Attribute, push or pull any object, or move up to Long Range from your body. The effect ends immediately and you return to your body upon interacting with any solid or liquid object, including rain or underwater environments.

## Nyctophilia Doctrine 
Utility. Force all Beings in Long Range to forget anything related to you (e.g., actions, the fact that they saw you, anything you said to them, etc.) from the last hour.
*“Every failure will eventually be forgotten in time.”*

## Where It Breaks
[ Pure 4d10 (min. 20) Aeon ]
Manifest a Devastating Blast towards an Being in Very Long Range.

## Multitude of You
Teleport to any tile in Very Long Range for Free for the next 3 rounds.

## Infinite Mass, Zero Density 
Manifest a Black Hole in Short Range. For the next 3 rounds, at the end of your turn, the Black Hole deals 10 Aeon damage to all Enemies in Medium Range of it. Instantly defeats any Enemy in Range with less than 15 HP.

## Rewrite Tomorrow 
Save your current HP and tile position. When Fading or before reaching negative Max HP, instantly rewind to your stored position, your HP is restored to the stored Value, and all Status Effects are removed. This Save Point lasts until the end of combat or scene.

## Vive le roi!
Until the end of your next turn, your Melee Weapon is replaced by Clarent:
### Clarent: Blade of The People 
[ 8 + LV Aeon | Slash Family | Long Range | 0 Upgrade Slots ]
Unstoppable: Range is set to Long instead of Melee.
Merciless: Attacks target all Enemies in Range at once.
Cursed: Take 8 + LV Aeon damage when this weapon disappears.

## Reaching Blindly
Become immune to all damage (except Aeon), until the end of your next turn.

## Legacy of White Cloth 
Manifest a Holy Banner that moves with you. Allies in Medium Range gain +15 to their Attack and Defense Sequences and cannot be moved by force. At the end of each of your turns, take 10 Aeon damage. Ends when you are Fading or when you choose.

## Against The Silence
Heal all Allies in Medium Range to Max HP and remove all Status Effects.

## Palace of Nostalgia
Manifest a closed Building, where you and another Being in Medium Range are taken.
While inside, outside Beings cannot interact with you and vice versa. The other Being brought inside this Building has only 2HB maximum Beats and cannot Move. The Building disappears when you desire or at the end of your next turn.
