### Exploit Influence (Tier VI) 3FB ●●●
Utility. Exhaust. Bring an object in Long Range towards you. Cannot be larger than yourself.
_"What is yours is simply mine."_

// Presentation Ideas
•     You snap your fingers and a localized wormhole drops the object perfectly into your waiting hand.
•     You cast an invisible, magnetic fishing line that hooks the item and yanks it violently through the air into your grip.
•     The object simply "forgets" its current location and blinks into existence within your grasp, accompanied by a popping sound.

### King of Glass (Tier VI) 6HB ●●●
Utility. Exhaust. Manifest an expanding Bridge of Glass, enabling movement across mid-air and negating all fall damage until the end of the scene. Each tile of glass left detonates when crossed by an Enemy, dealing 5 Entropy damage.
_"A path of fragility and ruin."_

// Presentation Ideas
•     Stained glass windowpanes assemble themselves under your sneakers, shattering explosively if pursuers step on them.
•     Neon grid-lines form a retro-futuristic walkway in the air, leaving explosive light-trails behind you.
•     You walk on floating, frozen water lilies that turn into superheated steam-bombs when touched by enemies.

### Origin of Vitriol (Tier VI) 6HB ●●●
**[ Varies Entropy ]**
Combative. Exhaust. Manifest 5 Crimson Knives for all Beings in Medium Range (up to 40). At the start of your next turn, as a Free action, stab a Being in Range with all the Knives. Each Knife deals 1 Entropy damage.
_"A thousand cuts born from a single, hateful thought."_

// Presentation Ideas
•     Five floating daggers made of crystallized, boiling blood track the targets with red laser-sights before launching in unison.
•     You tear a rift in space, revealing a dimension of pure malice from which five jagged, rusted scalpels shoot outward.
•     Skeletal, spectral hands emerge from the floor beneath the targets, each gripping a glowing ruby shiv that they violently thrust upward.

### Dying Star (Tier VI) 6HB ●●●
**[ Varies Entropy ]**
Exhaust. Combative. Manifest a Collapsing Star. Detonate the Star for Free to pull all Beings within Short Range toward it and apply Burst to each pulled Being. Immediately PROC Burst on all affected Beings.

### Transcendental Abyss  (Tier VI) 6HB ●●●
Exhaust. Swift. Manifest an Phantom Ocean that covers everything an every Being in Very Long Range. While inside the Ocean, Movement and Fall damage are halved (down), Ranged Attack Values lower than 20 automatically miss, and all fires are extinguished. The Ocean Disappears at the start of your next turn, at the end of the scene or when you choose.

### Kill Screen (Tier VI) 6HB ●●●
Exhaust. Manifest a Short Range Death Zone that lasts until the end of your next turn. While inside the Zone, any defeated Being gives their attacker +2HB.
