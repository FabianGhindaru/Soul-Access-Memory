
## Understanding Manifestations

A Manifestation's **Tier** equals its base Beat cost in Half-Beats — Tier II costs 2 HB, Tier IV costs 4 HB (2 FB), and so on.

Each additional Half-Beat spent beyond the base cost **Amplifies** the Manifestation by one step, adding +1 die to the attack roll unless the entry specifies a different Amplify effect. Amplifying without sufficient Harmonic Resonance skill deals Aeon damage to yourself equal to the maximum value of every die added.

Manifestations that can chain list dice, CF, and damage type. Their CF works identically to weapon CF when used as the follow-up in a Chain. 
**Manifestation attacks cannot Crit**.

[ 1d4 (CF 1) Arcane ]

Amplify: Gain +5 additional tiles to the push distance in addition to the die increase.

// Presentation Ideas

### Phantom Reach (Tier I) 1HB ◐
[ Utility ]
Increase the Range of your Melee Weapon to Short Range until the end of your next turn. 
Amplify: Increase its Range by an additional step (e.g., Short to Medium).
_"The boundary is thinner than the edge of your blade."_

// Presentation Ideas

•     You slash the air, sending a compressed vacuum blade flying forward.

### Synesthesia (Tier I) 1HB ◐
[ Utility ]
Manifest a visual, auditory, olfactory, tactile, or gustatory illusion of a known sensation upon a Being or object. The effect lasts until the end of the scene or until dismissed. Win a Soul Clash to convince a Being that the illusion is real. 
Amplify: No additional effect.

// Presentation Ideas
•     They suddenly taste pennies and blood, distracting them from reality.
•     A phantom swarm of buzzing bees surrounds their ears, entirely invisible.
•     The overwhelming scent of blooming jasmine completely masks everything else in the room.
