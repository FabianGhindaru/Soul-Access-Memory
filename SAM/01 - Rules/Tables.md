## Derived Checks

| Check | Formula | Used For |
|-------|---------|----------|
| **Grit** | (Body + Mind) / 2 | Stealth, crafting, surgery, lockpicking, endurance, precision work |
| **Aura** | (Body + Soul) / 2 | Dancing, animal handling, intimidation, rallying crowds, performance |
| **Will** | (Mind + Soul) / 2 | Deception, insight, persuasion, resisting manipulation, gambling |

Roll the dice pool for the resulting score (0-4: 1d6, 5-8: 2d6, 9-12: 3d6). Tiebreaker: higher of the two Attributes.

**Example:** Body 7 + Mind 4 -> Grit = (7+4)/2 = 5 -> 2d6 pool.
