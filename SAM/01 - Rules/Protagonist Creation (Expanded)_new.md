# Protagonist Creation — Expanded
This guide includes everything you need to create a Protagonist in just a few minutes.


## Step 0 — A Quick Note

> **The rules are a skeleton. The paint is yours.** Describe your abilities however you want. As long as the numbers and effects stay the same (still dealing X damage, still applying a status), the look, feel, and name are entirely up to you. Your Firestarter can be a swarm of embers, a shard of frozen light, or a music note that detonates on impact. The mechanics are the scaffolding. **How you envision your Protagonist is the art**.

**Example:** The spell *Firestarter* deals 2d4 Arcane damage and applies Burst. One player flicks a lit cigarette that detonates on the enemy's jacket. Another hums a dissonant chord: the note hangs in the air, then shatters against the enemy's skull. Two different Protagonists, two different worlds, one spell. 
**The dice don't care what color your fire is**.

---

## Step 1 — Who Are You?

Write one sentence about your character. A name, a job, a vibe, a regret. Examples:

> *"A disgraced medic who still patches people up even though her license was revoked."*
> *"A courier who's seen too many packages they weren't supposed to open."*
> *"An old syndicate cleaner trying to stay retired."*

This sentence is your anchor. When you're stuck later, come back to it.

---

## Step 2 — What Are You Made Of?

Divide **9 Attribute Points** among three traits (your Conductor may adjust this number). Write the number next to each. None can go above 12.

| Trait    | What It Does (in plain terms)                                                                      |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Body** | How tough you are. How much punishment you can take. How fast you move. How hard you hit up close. |
| **Mind** | How smart you are. How fast you react. How sharp your aim is. How well you interact people.        |
| **Soul** | How deep your power runs. How many spells you can cast. How lucky you are.                         |

**If you want to be...**
- A brawler, soldier, or tank → put your highest number in **Body**.
- A gunslinger, detective, or tactician → put your highest number in **Mind**.
- A mage, mystic, or healer → put your highest number in **Soul**.
- A jack-of-all-trades → spread them evenly.

---

## Step 3 — Pick Your Archetype

An Archetype is *how you fight*. Pick **one**. You can't change it later. Read the descriptions and choose what sounds cool. This decision is not as important as you may think.

Each archetype comes with **two core passives** (always active) and a pool of **combat abilities** (you equip 3). Below is one signature ability per archetype to give you a feel — this is not the full list.

---

### Caretaker
*Combat medic, guardian angel*

**You are good at:** Healing that hurts your enemies. Giving away pieces of yourself for the greater good.

**Core Passives:**
- *Nurturing Frenzy* — When you heal someone, everyone near them gets healed too. But if nobody needs healing by your next turn, you pay for it in blood.
- *Pays To Be Kind* — You're naturally gifted at treating injuries. Once per session, someone you helped in the past shows up when you need them most.

**Signature Ability — Disarming Remedy:** Create a circle on the ground. Enemies inside it are healed, but also get twisted and drained (bad status effects). A trap disguised as mercy.

---

### Daredevil
*Free runner, adrenaline junkie*

**You are good at:** Moving faster than anyone else. Your abilities cost nothing, you just go.

**Core Passives:**
- *Asphalt Scars* — Sacrifice your own health to move even faster. You can reverse this outside of combat.
- *Blink And I'm Gone* — Navigating obstacles comes naturally. Once per session, arrive somewhere you shouldn't be able to reach — a sealed floor, a moving vehicle — exactly when it matters.

**Signature Ability — Hex Drift:** After sprinting past an enemy, your next strike hits much harder. Movement is your wind-up.

---

### Dissident
*Revolutionary, agitator*

**You are good at:** Turning enemies against each other. Weaponizing crowds. Status effects.

**Core Passives:**
- *Stand Your Ground* — You're immune to status effects (but their triggered explosions still hurt you).
- *Vox Populi* — The city's forgotten trust you. Once per session, spark a riot, protest, or strike that locks down a district.

**Signature Ability — Radicalize:** Force an enemy to attack their own ally — and treat their strike as a critical hit.

---

### Eidolon
*Artist of the self, authentic to the bone*

**You are good at:** Being connected to yourself. Shields, warps, and strikes: you're never fighting alone.

**Core Passives:**
- *Hex Travel* — Reduce your own movement to extend your Voyager's reach.
- *Unfiltered Presence* — You can't be manipulated or gaslit. Once per session, walk into any restricted area and be granted passage — no questions asked.

**Keystone — Infinite Mirror:** You begin with the **Voyager**, a second manifestation of yourself. It takes any shape, fights independently, and you can command it from its position. Two bodies, one will.

**Signature Ability — Iso:** Leave your Voyager on a tile. At any point, teleport back to it — or swap places with it.

---

### Facilitator
*Invisible hand, stage builder*

**You are good at:** Making your allies shine. Granting them extra actions, protection, openings.

**Core Passives:**
- *Silk Network* — Your abilities can originate from any ally in range, not just yourself.
- *The Black Book* — Investigations come easily to you. Once per session, your contact gives you high-level intel: a bypass code, guard rotations, a target's current location.

**Signature Ability — Relative to Your Vision:** When an ally acts, command a second ally to immediately do the exact same thing — for free. If they can't, they gain a burst of energy instead.

---

### Professional
*Cold operative, perfectionist*

**You are good at:** Swapping weapons mid-fight. Ending enemies and chaining the kill into the next.

**Core Passives:**
- *Suppression Chain* — Defeating an enemy primes your next shot to automatically crit.
- *Smooth Operator* — Lies and manipulation come easily. You carry a Platinum Watch that can hide one small object from all searches.

**Keystone — Meticulous Reserve:** You carry extra weapons in reserve slots. Drawing from this personal arsenal is effortless.

**Signature Ability — End Shift:** After defeating an enemy, attack once with *every weapon you carry* in rapid succession — for free. Anything out of range grants you movement instead.

---

### Renegade
*Infamous legend, wildcard*

**You are good at:** Stances. You switch fighting styles mid-combat. Unpredictable and dangerous.

**Core Passives:**
- *Crowded Mind* — You start with three stances equipped instead of one.
- *Dark Alley Fame* — Your reputation precedes you. Once per session, your fans start a fight with local authorities or rival gangs creating opportunities for you.

**Keystone — Rebel Spirit:** Switching stances triggers an Entry effect (as you adopt the new stance) and a Switch effect (from the stance you leave). Each stance changes how you fight.

**Signature Ability — Stance: Desperado:** Entry: your next attack hits an additional target. Switch (when leaving): your next attack gains bonus damage.

---

### Sentinel
*Unyielding wall, personal code of justice*

**You are good at:** Stopping enemies in their tracks. Controlling territory. Making them fight you.

**Core Passives:**
- *Intercept* — When an enemy moves past you, stop them dead. Force them to target you next, and gain resistance to their damage type.
- *Unofficial Authority* — Intimidation and interrogation are second nature. Once per session, commandeer a vehicle, demand access to a restricted area, or requisition gear from security — they'll comply out of respect or fear.

**Signature Ability — Line In The Sand:** Create a physical barrier across 5 tiles. Enemies can't pass through or target allies on the other side. Anyone adjacent at the start of their turn takes damage. The barrier holds until you're near death or dismiss it.

---

### Zealot
*Devoted believer, unshakeable*

**You are good at:** Mixing serums for every situation. One mission a healer, the next a walking hazard.

**Core Passives:**
- *Fear of Separation* — Your healing on dying allies becomes especially potent, but you can't be healed yourself while this is active.
- *True Believer* — You can't be coerced or manipulated. Once per session, declare your conviction to an overwhelming force — they'll hesitate, back down, or grant you a major concession.

**Signature Ability — Aurora Serum:** Apply shimmering stacks to a target. Stack twice on an ally for a massive heal that splashes to everyone nearby. Stack twice on an enemy for an Entropy explosion.

## Step 4 — Pick Your Weapons

You carry **one Melee** and **one Ranged** weapon at all times. Pick one family from each column. The family gives your weapon a special trick.

Your weapon's **quality** determines its baseline power. If unsure, pick Basic.

| Quality | Bonus | Who Has This |
|---------|:-----:|--------------|
| **Basic** | +0 | Street-level, scrap-built, fresh out of the academy. |
| **Refined** | +5 | Tuned, quality craftsmanship, a professional's tool. |
| **Masterpiece** | +10 | Heirloom, custom-forged, a legend's weapon. Rare. |

### Melee (pick one)

| Family | Examples | Special Trick |
|--------|----------|---------------|
| **Blunt** | Hammers, clubs, batons | Knock enemies back on hit. |
| **Slash** | Swords, knives, claws | Cause bleeding on a perfect strike. |
| **Pierce** | Spears, rapiers, needles | Damage splashes to enemies standing next to your target. |
| **Thrown** | Javelins, axes, shuriken | Throw it — it comes back. |
| **Lash** | Whips, chains | Longer reach than other melee weapons. |
| **Brawl** | Gauntlets, brass knuckles, fists | Your combos chain together better than anyone else's. |

### Ranged (pick one)

| Family     | Examples                      | Special Trick                                          |
| ---------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Pistol** | Handguns, sidearms            | Drawing it gives you a free shot.                      |
| **SMG**    | Submachine guns               | Extra bullet per burst — but wild shots can betray you. |
| **Rifle**  | Battle rifles, carbines       | Steady aim: if you don't move first, you hit harder.   |
| **Sniper** | High-caliber precision rifles | Perfect shots hit much harder than anyone else's.      |
| **Heavy**  | Miniguns, grenade launchers   | Your shots explode, hitting everyone near the target.  |
| **Exotic** | Custom, weird, one-of-a-kind  | Ask your Conductor.                                    |

## Step 5 — Pick Your Manifestations (Spells)

Manifestations are spells. How many you know depends on your **Soul** (from Step 2). Higher Soul unlocks higher Tiers.

| Soul | You Know | Tiers Available |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| 0 | 0 spells | None |
| 1-2 | 1-2 spells | Tier I only (simple tricks) |
| 3-5 | 3-5 spells | Tiers I–III (combat-ready magic) |
| 6+ | 6+ spells | Tiers I–VI (full power) |

### Quick Picks by Playstyle

**Hurt things:**
- *Firestarter* (Tier I) — Set someone on fire. Amplify for more dice.
- *Psy Knife* (Tier II) — Psychic blade. If it kills, it jumps to the next target for free.
- *Feels Like Murder* (Tier II) — Summon eclipse crows. Expend them as attack dice or chain fuel. Applies Drain.
- *Pulverize Their Bones* (Tier V) — Pour your own life into a devastating strike. If it kills, excess damage heals you.

**Protect people:**
- *Faint Smile* (Tier II) — Heal an ally. Amplify to double the effect.
- *Always Faithful* (Tier II) — Remove a harmful status effect. Amplify to remove a second.
- *Unbreachable Heart* (Tier II) — Raise pillars of stone as a wall. Block movement and vision.
- *Sanguine Contingency* (Tier V) — Instantly swap HP with a willing ally.

**Control the battlefield:**
- *Force Projection* (Tier I) — Push someone across the room. Amplify for more distance.
- *Mind Labyrinth* (Tier II) — Trap an enemy in their own head. Applies Twist and freezes their movement.
- *Permanent Reminder* (Tier II) — Brand an enemy with a warding tattoo. If they attack the owner, you take Aeon (unpreventable) damage, but they must pick a different target.
- *Touch of Madness* (Tier V) — Make someone follow your command. Won't do anything against their core values.

**Get weird:**
- *Phantasmagoria* (Tier I) — Your shadow detaches and does one thing: move an object, pull a lever, or attack.
- *Synesthesia* (Tier I) — Make someone taste copper, smell smoke, hear bees. Win a Soul clash to make them believe it's real.
- *Seasons of Change* (Tier II) — Summon cursed mirrors. Shatter one to rewrite a defense roll — turn an enemy's best die into a 1, or an ally's worst into its maximum.
- *Flick-Flack* (Tier IV) — Instantly swap places with anyone.

## Step 6 — Pick Your Flow State

A Flow State is your ultimate move: a reality-bending ability fueled by a rare substance called **Noesis**. You pick one at Level 1. Only one Flow State can be activated per round, and it costs nothing to trigger.

| Flow State                      | What Happens                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **How Life Flows**              | Summon a personal storm for 3 rounds. Each round you get three free actions: push enemies with wind, strike with lightning, pelt with hail.    |
| **Astral Projection**           | Leave your body. Time freezes. Take one perfect action — attack for massive damage, move anywhere, or manipulate an object — then return.      |
| **Nyctophilia Doctrine**        | Everyone in range forgets everything about you from the last hour. Footage corrupts. Witnesses blank. You were never there.                    |
| **Where It Breaks**             | A single devastating blast of pure Aeon (unpreventable) damage at extreme range. Unblockable, unmitigatable. Minimum 20 damage.                |
| **Multitude of You**            | For 3 rounds, teleport anywhere as a free action. You're everywhere and nowhere.                                                               |
| **Infinite Mass, Zero Density** | Create a black hole. For 3 rounds it pulses Aeon (unpreventable) damage to all nearby enemies. Instantly executes anyone in range below 15 HP. |
| **Rewrite Tomorrow**            | Save your current HP and position. If you die before combat ends, rewind — restore HP, position, and cleanse all status effects.               |
| **Vive le roi!**                | Replace your melee weapon with Clarent, a cursed sword with massive range that hits everyone at once. You take damage when it fades.           |
| **Reaching Blindly**            | Become immune to all damage except Aeon for one full turn.                                                                                     |
| **Legacy of White Cloth**       | Raise a holy banner. Allies near you gain massive attack and defense buffs and can't be moved. You take Aeon damage each turn it flies.        |
| **Against The Silence**         | Heal all allies in range to full HP and remove every status effect. A complete reset.                                                          |
| **Palace of Nostalgia**         | Drag one enemy into a pocket dimension. Inside, they can barely move or act. Outside, nobody can interact with either of you.                  |
