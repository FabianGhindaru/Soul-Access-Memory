You track two currencies as **exact numbers** on your character sheet. 

*   **Syn-C:** Digital credit tied to your Handler. Abundant and accepted everywhere in the Bloom, but entirely traceable.
*   **Kaph:** Heavy, untraceable, pre-Collapse physical currency. Required for black market deals and surviving the underground.


---

### Lifestyle Thresholds (The Ledger)
To keep the game moving, you don't need to track the cost of every synthetic coffee or subway ride—unless you are genuinely broke. Your current wealth dictates your **Lifestyle Threshold**. 

Add your Syn-C and Kaph together to find your Tier. **Any purchase below your Tier’s limit is "free"**—absorbed by your daily budget. You do not deduct it from your sheet.

| Tier | Total Wealth | The Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tight** | **0 – 249** | You track every penny. Even a 5 Syn-C meal is deducted from your sheet. |
| **Stable** | **250 – 1,999** | You are comfortable. **Purchases of 25 or less are free.** You eat well and travel easily without checking your balance. |
| **Flush** | **2,000+** | You have significant resources. **Purchases of 100 or less are free.** You buy rounds for the bar and tip generously. |

*Note: If a large purchase drops your total wealth into a lower tier, you immediately lose the perks of the higher tier. The luxury ends until your next payday.*

### The Rainform Rule
When the dome opens and the city shifts into **Rainform Mode**, the Handler enters SafeMode. **Syn-C commerce completely suspends.** 

During the rain, only Kaph works. If you have 5,000 Syn-C but 0 Kaph when the storm hits, you are effectively broke. You cannot even buy a drink in a Rain Lounge. Furthermore, deep in the Roots, Syndicate dealers will often refuse Syn-C entirely for Major purchases, demanding physical Kaph to avoid Machina tracking. Keep some coins in your pocket.
