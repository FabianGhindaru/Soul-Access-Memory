### Flick-Flack (Tier IV) 2FB ●●
**[ 5d6 (CF 12) Arcane ]**
Harmless. Cadence. Swap places with a Being. 
_"I saw myself for a brief moment."_

// Presentation Ideas
•     Two massive, ethereal playing cards appear over you and the target, shuffling instantly to swap your positions in a burst of confetti.
•     A spatial rift shaped like a zipper opens; you dive in, and the target is forcibly spat out where you just were.
•     A sudden, dizzying camera-flash blinds everyone; when the spots clear, you and the target are standing in each other's footprints.

### Monarch Blade (Tier IV) 2FB ●●
**[ Varies (CF 8) Arcane ]**
Combative. Manifest a Butterfly Sword until the end of your current turn. Perform a Free Attack with the Sword against a Being in Range using 1d6. If the result is 2 or higher, add an additional d6 to the Attack Roll. Repeatable up to 6 times.
Amplify: Decrease the minimum required roll by 1 (e.g., from 2-6 to 1-6). Repeatable once.
_"Iridescent flickers sing about The Emperor."_

// Presentation Ideas
•     Swings manifest as trails of golden monarch butterflies that shred armor like glass.
•     You summon an ornate, floating sword that duplicates itself geometrically with every successful strike.
•     Your arm blurs into six simultaneous afterimages, each landing a cascading blow in a different colored light.

### Overcharge (Tier IV) 2FB ●●
**[ 4d6 (CF 10) Arcane ]**
Exhaust. Overflow. Heal and apply a Status Effect of your choosing on you. First attack next turn, also applies the chosen Status Effect.
_"Setbacks and opportunities differ based on your mentality."_

// Presentation Ideas
•     You jam a literal, glowing syringe of pure adrenaline into your chest, your veins pulsing with blinding, volatile neon light.
•     You inhale a deeply glowing, toxic-looking vapor that forcefully knits your wounds but makes your eyes burn with manic energy.
•     A localized lightning bolt strikes you directly; instead of burning you, it supercharges your body, leaving you violently crackling with power.

### Shrouded Veritas (Tier IV) 2FB ●●
**[ 2d8 (CF 7) Arcane ]**
Combative. Attack a Being. A successful hit applies 🌀Twist.
Swift. Until the end of the target's next turn, when they declare an attack, roll 1d6 for Free: on a 5–6, they attack a random Being in range instead.
Amplify: Decrease the minimum required roll by 1 (e.g., from 5–6 to 4–6).
_"The truth is often the most misunderstood lie."_

// Presentation Ideas
•     You slap them with a spectral blindfold that fundamentally warps their sense of friend and foe.
•     A shimmering mist enters their ears, whispering paranoid lies that make them turn on their allies.
•     You hit them with a mirror shard; their reflection turns around and forces them to attack the wrong person.

### Regal Impasse (Tier IV) 2FB ●●
**[ Varies Melee | Summoning ]**
Cadence. Manifest 6 Ethereal Swords that swirl around you until the end of your next turn. Expend any number of Swords to Attack a Being, each Sword contributing +5 to the Attack Roll. Alternatively, expend Swords to Chain: add +3 CF per Sword to the Attack Sequence. Successful hits pushes the Being 3 tiles for each Sword used.
Amplify: No additional effect.
