## Archetype


**Silk Network**
Passive. When one of your attacks, abilities or Manifestations references a Range Band, you may measure that range from yourself or from any Ally within Medium Range of you. This applies to every Facilitator ability as well. Line of sight from the chosen origin is still required where it matters.

**The Black Book**
Once per session, call a Contact to gain one specific piece of high-level intel from anywhere in the city: a hidden security bypass code, the current guard rotation, the exact location of a person currently in transit and so on. This works regardless of Grid Signal.

**1. Relative to Your Vision (Free)**
Exhaust. Swift. When an Ally within Long Range of you completes an action costing 1 Beat or less, you may command a second Ally within Long Range of the first to immediately perform the same action at no Beat cost, using their own values and gear and choosing their own target. If the second Ally cannot legally duplicate the action, they instead gain 1 Beat to spend before the end of this Round.

**2. Setting the Stage (Free)**
Cadence. When you hand the Spotlight to another Protagonist, choose one benefit for them: +5 to their first Attack Value this Round, or +5 to their first Defense roll this Round.

**3. Cognitive Overload (1 Beat)**
Exhaust. All Allies within Short Range of you (excluding yourself) gain 1 additional Beat, available until the start of the next Player Phase. At the start of the next Player Phase, each of those Allies takes 10 Entropy damage.

**Special: The Big Play (1 Beat)**
Exhaust. Choose one action: a basic attack, a Lesser Manifestation, using an item, stabilizing an ally, Hiding, moving up to 6 tiles, or a comparable simple action (Conductor's call). Every Ally within Very Long Range may immediately perform that action once at no Beat cost, each choosing their own target, direction, and execution. Allies do not need the Spotlight or remaining Beats to do this.


## Skills

**Vigilance (Free)** — Mind 2 · MP 2
Cadence. Declare at the start of your Spotlight. Forfeit all Movement this Player Phase. Gain +5 MDV and +5 DDV until the next Player Phase.

**Suppressive Fire (1 Beat)** — Mind 4 · MP 4
Cadence. Select a point in Medium Range within your Ranged weapon's fictionally established range. Line of sight required to the point. Create a zone of Short Range radius around that point until the start of your next Spotlight. Your Spotlight ends. Enemies in the zone suffer −10 AV.

**All Skill** — Will 4 · MP 6
1/Session. Succeed on a Skill Check requiring 2 Hits or fewer without rolling. Does not work on Attacks, defenses, Versus Checks, or pure-dice rolls.

**Timing x2** — MP 1 each
Passive. Add +1 to your Mind for Spotlight selection only.
