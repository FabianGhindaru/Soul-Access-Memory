
# CORE MECHANICS

> **Protagonist Level:** `(Body + Mind + Soul) ÷ 3`, rounded down.
> **Attribute Modifier:** `Attribute Score ÷ 2`, rounded up. *(e.g., Body 9 → Modifier 5)*

## Currencies
*   **AP (Attribute Points):** Spent to raise Body, Mind, and Soul directly. Earned through leveling. Attributes range from 0–12 and gate skill access.
*   **MP (Mnemonic Points):** Spent to purchase Enhancements, Techniques, and Aptitudes. Earned by completing missions.

## How to Unlock Abilities
Unless otherwise noted, all abilities can be purchased with **MP** at any time, provided you meet the Attribute Gate. 

**Campaign Unlocks:** Certain abilities can *also* be acquired narratively for free. The Conductor awards the ability when the specific in-fiction condition is met. You can always bypass the narrative requirement by paying the MP cost.
