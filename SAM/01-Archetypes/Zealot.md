### Zealot
_"For those who believe that loyalty and effort are intertwined."_

The Zealot chose something — a syndicate, a doctrine, a person, an idea — and they chose it completely. Not out of blind obedience, but because they understand what it means to stand for nothing, and they refuse it. They are the one who shows up, who doesn't waver when everyone else starts calculating exits. This makes them invaluable and dangerous in equal measure. The question is never whether the Zealot will hold the line. The question is whether the cause they're holding it for deserves them.

_[Loyal & Unwavering]_


----------- CORE PASSIVES -----------------

**Fear of Separation (○)****
Exhaust. Until the end of your next turn, any healing you provide to Fading Allies gains Overflow. While this ability is active, you cannot be healed by any source. If you start Fading, this ability ends early.

**True Believer**
Passive. You are immune to coercion, manipulation, or other influences against your cause.
Once per session, declare your absolute conviction to an overwhelming hostile force or authority figure. They will hesitate, back down, or grant a major concession out of sheer unease at your complete lack of self-preservation.


----------- ARCHETYPE KEYSTONE ---------------

**Golden Hours**
You have access to all the Serums listed below. Choose 3 Serums to use as your active combat abilities. You can freely swap your active Serums for any other Serum whenever you are out of combat.

At levels 1, 3, and 6, you empower one of your Serums instead of gaining a new ability.

Serums are applied for Free to a damage source (such as weapons or Manifestations), and their effects trigger on a successful hit on a Being. Applying Serums on the Zealot don’t require a damage source

As a Sub-Archetype, choose 1 Serum instead, which becomes permanent.


----------- COMBAT ABILITIES -----------------

**Dawn Serum**
Harmless. Heal a Being for 1d10.
OR
Exhaust. Heal a Being for 7.
Empower: Increase the healing by an additional 10.

**Noon Serum**
Exhaust. Apply 🩸Drain or 🔥Burst.
Empower: Apply 🩸Drain, 🔥Burst, or 🌀️Twist.

**Dusk Serum**
Exhaust. Gain immunity to Entropy Damage until the start of your next turn.

**Twilight Serum**
Exhaust. Create a Short Range “Noxious Cloud” that deals 10 Entropy damage to any Enemy that starts its turn inside it. Disappears at the end of your next turn. The Cloud is centered around the Being it was afflicted on.
Empower: Increase the Range to Medium and it deals 15 Entropy damage instead.

**Midnight Serum**
Exhaust. Create a Short Range “Ventablack Mist” that increases Defense Values of all Beings inside it by 15. Disappears at the start of your next turn. The Mist is centered around the Being it was afflicted on.
Empower: Gives resistance to all damage types instead.

**Aurora Serum**
Harmless. Apply 1 stack of “Shimmer” to a Being. Applying another stack of Shimmer to a Being with 2 or more stacks triggers an effect based on the target: on Allies, heal for 15 and all Allies in Short Range heal for 10; on Enemies, deal 20 Entropy damage and all Enemies in Short Range take 15 Entropy damage. Empower: Increase the primary effect to 25 and the splash effect to 20 for both Allies and Enemies.

**Eventide Serum**
Exhaust. Harmless. Gain 1 Half Beat and 10 Movement.
Empower: Gain 1 Full Beat and 15 Movement instead.

**Special: Witching Hour (●)**
End your current turn. Refresh all exhausted Zealot Serums.
