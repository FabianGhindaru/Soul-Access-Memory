### Daredevil
_"For those who believe that slowing down feels like dying."_

The Daredevil is velocity incarnate. Leaping from rooftop to rooftop, they refuse to be held still. Fueled by adrenaline and a total disregard for consequences, the Daredevil may be the only truly free soul in the city.
Yet one question remains: is this freedom authentic, or simply a way to outrun their own insecurities? Time will see if moving faster than their demons is a viable choice.
_[Free & Reckless]_


----------- CORE PASSIVES -----------------

**Asphalt Scars**
Sacrifice up to half your Max HP to gain that much Max Movement. This trade-off can only be reversed while you are out of combat.

**Blink And I’m Gone**
Passive. Checks related to navigating physical space, regardless of complexity, height, or apparent impassability start with 1 Hit.
Once per session, arrive somewhere that shouldn't be reachable — a sealed floor, a moving vehicle, a collapsing structure — at the exact moment your presence matters most. No roll or explanation required.


----------- ARCHETYPE KEYSTONE ---------------

**No Time To Think**
Passive. All Daredevil’s combat abilities are Free.
Applies to Sub-Archetypes as well.
_[Skirmisher / Striker]_

----------- COMBAT ABILITIES -----------------

**1. Joyride**
Cadence. When you move this turn, you may take a willing Ally in Melee Range with you. When your movement ends, place them in a tile adjacent to you.

**2. Carbon Sting**
Exhaust. Successfully hitting an Enemy allows you to spend all your current remaining Movement to push them a number of tiles equal to the Movement spent in a direction of your choice (max. 15 tiles).
Defeating an Enemy with the push damage, Refreshes this ability.

**3. Hex Drift**
Cadence. After moving at least 6 tiles while remaining in Short Range of an Enemy, gain a +10 bonus to your next Attack Value against them this turn. For multiple Enemies, choose only one.

**4. Slipstream**
Passive. Passing by an Enemy in Melee Range gives you +5 Movement this turn, up to a maximum of +15.

**Special: //The Pretender** 
Exhaust. Swift. Gain a passive Dodge Defense Value equal to twice your Max Movement that lasts until the start of your next turn.
