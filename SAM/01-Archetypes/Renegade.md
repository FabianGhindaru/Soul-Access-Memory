### Renegade
_"For those who don’t believe in the lie of peace."_

The Renegade is a legend first, flesh second. They carve through the city’s underground, leaving a wake of scorched exits and weaponized rumor. They know exactly what they are building.: a violently guarded autonomy.
The underworld is paralyzed, unsure whether to crown them or bury them. To the Renegade, that hesitation is exactly the space they need to operate entirely on their own terms.


----------- CORE PASSIVES -----------------

**Crowded Mind**
Start with three combat abilities (Stances), instead of one. Each time you have entered all your equipped Stances, Refresh Devil’s Roulette.

**Dark Alley Fame**
Passive. Your starting bounty is increased by 1. Checks related to hiding or escaping start with 1 hit.
Once per session, your fans can start a fight with local authorities, rival syndicates, or any other Enemy group. If used in combat, they deal LV * Bounty Melee Damage to all Enemies in Long Range.


----------- ARCHETYPE KEYSTONE ---------------

**Rebel Spirit (○)**
Cadence. Change your current Stance to a different one. Each Stance has an Entry when you adopt it and a Switch when you leave it. These abilities are passive and trigger automatically, but only apply to the current turn. 
Sub-Archetype: Gain 2 Stances instead.



----------- COMBAT ABILITIES -----------------

**[ Stance: Desperado ]**
Entry: Your next attack may target an additional Being in range.
Switch: Gain +5 to your next Attack Value.

**[ Stance: Maverick ]**
Entry: Increase the range of your next attack by one step (e.g., from Short -> Medium).
Switch: All Enemies in Short Range lose 10 Movement.

**[ Stance: Outlaw ]**
Entry: Your next successful attack hits all other Beings within Short Range of your target for the same amount as Entropy damage (Max. 5).
Switch: Your next attack's CF is doubled for chaining purposes.

**[ Stance: Rogue ]**
Entry: Gain +3 Movement for each Enemy in Melee Range (Max. +12 Movement).
Switch: If you've defeated an Enemy this turn, gain +5 to your Attack Value.

**[ Stance: Scoundrel ]**
Entry: Remove one Status Effect currently on you.
Switch: Your next successful attack applies Drain.

**[ Stance: Marauder]**
Entry: Your next successful attack heals you for the damage dealt (no Overflow).
Switch: Gain +1 to your next Attack Value for each Enemy in Melee Range.
