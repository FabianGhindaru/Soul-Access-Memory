### Eidolon
_"For those who believe you can arrive at yourself and stay there."_

The Eidolon knows exactly who they are. The good parts, the bad parts, the parts they'd rather not name out loud. They don't guess. They don't drift. People remember them not because they were mysterious, but because they were truly themselves. They are an artist whose medium is their own existence.

_[Ethereal & Authentic]_


----------- CORE PASSIVES -----------------

**Hex Travel**
Reduce your Max Movement by 3 to increase your Voyager’s Range by one step (e.g., Short to Medium). This reduction reverses only while out of combat.

**Unfiltered Presence**
Passive. You are immune to any attempts to manipulate your sense of self or your convictions.
Once per session, walk into any restricted area, hostile standoff, or exclusive gathering exactly as you are. Instead of challenging you, everyone present will inexplicably grant you free passage. Performing harmful actions will stop this effect.



----------- ARCHETYPE KEYSTONE ---------------

**Infinite Mirror**
The Eidolon begins with the Voyager, a unique Manifestation of yourself. The Voyager takes any shape desired, appears or disappears at will, and assists in both combat and narrative scenes. In combat, their abilities default to Short Range and the Voyager occupies an adjacent tile while following the Eidolon. The Voyager can be moved in their specific Range, and Attacks are considered weapon-less, but use the Soul Modifier instead.


----------- COMBAT ABILITIES -----------------

**Alt (●)**
Detach the Voyager to shield a Fading Ally. The Voyager remains near the Ally and doubles the Ally’s Mitigation or Dodge Defense Rolls. The effect ends when the Eidolon decides to end it or when the Ally is no longer Fading. Eidolon abilities cannot be used while this is active.
Althea: Triple the Ally’s Mitigation or Dodge Defense Rolls instead.

**Iso (○)**
Cadence. Leave the Voyager on your current tile.
Teleport back to the Voyager’s position for Free.
Isolde: Choose to teleport or switch places instead.

**Leo (●)**
Exhaust. Gain Immunity to all Ranged Attack Values below 25 until the start of your next turn.
Leocadia: Additionally, gain resistance to Melee and Arcane damage.

**Oph (◐)**
Cadence. Command your Voyager to follow an Ally in Short Range until the end of your next turn, or when you decide to stop this ability. 
Push the Ally up to 6 tiles towards your tile for Free.
Ophelia: Push the Ally up to 6 tiles in any
direction instead.

**Special: //Ismene** 
Passive. Upgrade all Eidolon combat abilities to their true power.
Alt becomes Althea.
Iso becomes Isolde.
Leo becomes Leocadia.
Oph becomes Ophelia.
Ism becomes Ismene.
