### Caretaker
_”For those who believe that keeping someone alive is an act of defiance.“_

The Caretaker operates where the city's light doesn't reach: among the sick, the discarded, and those left to rot by the syndicates and the state. To the desperate, they are a walking sanctuary, a rare beacon of light in an otherwise suffocating dark. They restore hope to people who have been taught they have no value. But this compassion is a demanding, dangerous discipline. To protect the vulnerable, the Caretaker will weaponize their own vitality, sacrificing themselves piece by piece to ensure the shadows never claim those under their protection.

----------- CORE PASSIVES -----------------

**Nurturing Frenzy (○)**
Exhaust. Combative. Swift. Activate this mode. While active, healing a Being also restores all Beings within Short Range for the same amount. If no HP is restored by the start of the next turn: this mode ends and take half of current HP as Aeon damage.

**Pays To Be Kind**
Passive. Checks related to injuries and adverse effects start with 1 Hit.
Once per session, a Being you helped before appears in a moment of crisis to offer aid.

----------- ARCHETYPE KEYSTONE ---------------

**Martyr’s Wish (○)**
Combative. Overflow. End Nurturing Frenzy voluntarily. Suffer all standard penalties associated with the mode ending prematurely. Restore HP to all Allies (except yourself) within Medium Range equal to the total HP lost during this termination.


----------- COMBAT ABILITIES -----------------

**Disarming Remedy (●)**
Create a Medium Range “Circle Of Remedy” in Short Range. All Enemies that start their turn in this the zone heal 10 HP but gain 🌀️Twist and 🩸Drain.
The Circle lasts until the start of your next turn.

**Kindness for Weakness (○)**
Exhaust. Until the end of your next turn, any Enemy you heal gets -10 to their next Defense Value.

**Throwing Away Oneself (●)**
Overflow. The next time you heal a Being, you may take Aeon damage equal to the base healing to double it.

**Covered By Light (●)**
Exhaust. Until the end of the current turn, increase the Range of any ability that targets a specific Range by one step for every 2 Beings within that Range.

**Special: //The Chain** 
Exhaust. Overflow. Select a Being in Short Range and alternate between an Ally and an Enemy up to 5 times. Ensure each subsequent Being is within Short Range of the last. Heal 10 HP to each Ally and deal 5 Entropy damage to each Enemy in the chain.
