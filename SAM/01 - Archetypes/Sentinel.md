### Sentinel
*"For those who believe that integrity doesn't wait for permission."*

The Sentinel draws their own lines and holds them. Their sense of justice is personal, precise, and often at odds with whatever the official verdict happens to be. They've made peace with operating in that gap, acting on what they believe is right and absorbing the friction that comes with it. The city's power structures alternate between resenting them and quietly relying on them. The Sentinel's position on this has remained consistent.


----------- CORE PASSIVES -----------------

**Intercept (Free)**
Cadence. Swift. When an Enemy moves through an adjacent tile, stop that Being's movement. Compel that Enemy to target you with its next Attack if able. Gain Resistance to one damage type of that Enemy's attack.

**Unofficial Authority**  
Passive. Checks related to intimidation or interrogation start with 1 Hit.  
Once per session, commandeer a vehicle, demand access to a restricted area, or requisition basic gear from law enforcement or corporate security. They will comply out of respect, fear, or a desire to avoid a physical confrontation.


----------- COMBAT ABILITIES -----------------

**Retribution (1 Beat)**
Combative. Mark an Enemy in Short Range until end of your next turn. The marked Enemy takes 5 Entropy damage each time it targets a Being other than you with an Attack.

**Bastion (2 Beats)**
Exhaust. Until start of your next turn, gain Resistance to all damage (except Aeon). All Allies in Melee Range gain Resistance to the same types. Enemies in Melee Range cannot move away from you without passing a Body versus check.

**Protector's Oath (4 Beats)**
Exhaust. Swift. Choose an Ally in Medium Range. Until start of your next turn, redirect all damage dealt to that Ally to yourself instead. While active, your Mitigate and Dodge refresh at the start of each of your turns (instead of the normal 1/round limit).

**Special: Line In The Sand (2 Beats)**
Exhaust. Create a physical barrier across 6 contiguous tiles within Medium Range. Prevent Enemies from moving through these tiles or targeting Allies on the opposite side. Any Enemy adjacent to the barrier at the start of their turn takes 10 Entropy Damage. Maintain until Fading begins or dismissed for Free.
