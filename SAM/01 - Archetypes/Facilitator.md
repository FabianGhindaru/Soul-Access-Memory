### Facilitator
_"For those who understand that the best work is invisible."_

The Facilitator doesn't need the spotlight. They need the mission to work. They see what each person in the room could become if given the right opening, the right nudge, the right moment and they give it. They clear the path, handle the friction, make the hard calls in the background. People walk away feeling like heroes. The Facilitator walks away knowing they built the stage.


----------- CORE PASSIVES -----------------

**Silk Network**  
Passive. Measure the range of abilities from yourself or an Ally within Medium Range.

**The Black Book**
Passive. Checks related to investigations start with 1 hit.
Once per session, call your Contact to gain a specific piece of high-level intel from anywhere in the city like a hidden security bypass code, the current guard rotation, or the exact location of a person currently in transit.


----------- COMBAT ABILITIES -----------------

**Channel Switch (2 Beats)**
Cadence. Grant Resistance to either Melee, Ranged, or Arcane damage to all Allies in Melee Range until the start of your next turn.

**Relative to Your Vision (2 Beats)** 
Exhaust. Swift. Target an Ally within Long Range who has just performed an Action. Command a second Ally within Long Range of the first to immediately perform that exact same Action for Free. Grant the second Ally 2 Beats immediately if they are unable to duplicate the Action.

**Cognitive Overload (Free)** 
Exhaust. Grant 2 Beats to all Allies in Medium Range to use on their next turn. Deal 10 Entropy damage to those Allies for each unused Beat they have remaining at the end of that turn.

**Cancel Your Plans (2 Beats)** 
Swift. Redirect half of the damage (down) taken by an Ally in Medium Range to yourself or another willing Ally in Medium Range.  
(2 Beats) Exhaust. Overflow. Heal the original Ally for an amount equal to the redirected damage.

**Special: The Big Play (2 Beats)**
Exhaust. Grant a Free Action of your choice (such as Attack, Move 6 Tiles, Push/Pull, or Activate) to every Ally within Very Long Range. Allow each Ally to determine the specific target, direction, or execution of that action immediately.  
(2 Beats) Grant one additional Free Action to a specific Ally. Repeatable.
