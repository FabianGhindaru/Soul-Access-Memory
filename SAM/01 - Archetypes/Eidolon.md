
## Eidolon

_"For those who believe you can arrive at yourself and stay there."_

The Eidolon knows exactly who they are. The good parts, the bad parts, the parts they'd rather not name out loud. They don't guess. They don't drift. People remember them not because they were mysterious, but because they were truly themselves. They are an artist whose medium is their own existence.

In combat, the Eidolon stages the Voyager before committing their own body, striking from Reach while the projection holds the forward position. Iso and Oph reposition endangered allies without spending their actions, Leo answers focused Ranged fire with immunity, and Alt detaches the Voyager to shield a Fading ally. Ismene rewards patience: every ability kept until its true version lands hits harder and reaches further.

**Fantasy:** Ethereal & Authentic
**Combat Style:** Coordinator / Skirmisher

### Innate Characteristics

**Hex Travel**
Reduce your Max Movement by 3 to increase that Voyager's Range by one step (e.g. Short to Medium). End that reduction outside combat.

**Unfiltered Presence**
Once per session, walk into any restricted area, hostile standoff, or exclusive gathering exactly as you are. Instead of challenging you, everyone present will inexplicably grant you free passage. Performing harmful actions will stop this effect.

### Archetype Keystone

**Infinite Mirror**
The Eidolon begins with the Voyager, a unique Manifestation of themselves. The Voyager takes any reasonable shape desired, appears or disappears at will, and assists in both combat and narrative scenes.

In combat, besides the combat abilities, that Voyager can Attack an Enemy in Short Range for 1 Beat. Deal Arcane Damage equal to double your Level. That Voyager cannot be targeted and does not count as a Being.

Outside combat, the Voyager can be used to interact with objects or people. It has the same strength and voice as you.

### Combat Abilities

**1. Alt (1 Beat)**
Detach that Voyager to shield a Fading Ally in Short Range. That Voyager remains near that Ally and adds +10 to that Ally's MDV. The effect ends when you decide to end it (Free) or when that Ally is no longer Fading. You cannot use Eidolon abilities while this is active.
Althea: Add +20 to that Ally's MDV instead.

**2. Iso (Free)**
Cadence. Leave that Voyager on your current tile. Teleport back to that Voyager's position for Free later this Spotlight.
Isolde: Teleport or switch places with that Voyager instead.

**3. Leo (1 Beat)**
Exhaust. Gain Immunity to Ranged Attacks below 20 AV, or grant that Immunity to an Ally in Short Range, until the start of the next Player Phase.
Leocadia: Set that Immunity threshold to below 40 AV instead.

**4. Oph (Free)**
Cadence. Command that Voyager to track an Ally in Short Range until the end of the next Player Phase or until dismissed. Push that Ally up to 8 tiles toward you for Free.
Ophelia: Push that Ally up to 8 tiles in any direction instead.

**Special: //Ismene (Passive)**
Passive. Upgrade all Eidolon combat abilities to their true power. Alt becomes Althea. Iso becomes Isolde. Leo becomes Leocadia. Oph becomes Ophelia. Ism becomes Ismene.
