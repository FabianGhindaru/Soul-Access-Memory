## Daredevil
_"For those who believe that slowing down feels like dying."_

The Daredevil is velocity incarnate. Leaping from rooftop to rooftop, they refuse to be held still. Fueled by adrenaline and a total disregard for consequences, the Daredevil believes that they are the only truly free soul in the city.
Yet one question remains: is this freedom authentic, or simply a way to outrun their own insecurities? Time will see if moving faster than their demons is a viable choice.

In combat, the Daredevil moves first and attacks second, scouting a route through threat ranges so Slipstream and Hex Drift pay for the strike. Carbon Sting turns leftover Movement into a long push, and a push that defeats its target funds the next attack. Joyride carries an ally out of danger along the same path, while The Pretender answers focused fire by converting raw speed into defense until the next Player Phase.

**Fantasy:** Free & Reckless
**Combat Style:** Skirmisher / Striker

### Innate Characteristics

**Asphalt Scars**
At the start of combat, you may halve your starting HP. If you do, double your Max Movement until the end of combat.

**Blink And I’m Gone**
Once per session, arrive somewhere that shouldn't be reachable such as a sealed floor, a moving vehicle, or collapsing structure. No roll or explanation required.

### Archetype Keystone

**No Time To Think**
Passive. All Daredevil’s combat abilities are Free.
Applies as a Sub-Archetype as well.

### Combat Abilities


**1. Joyride (Free)**
Cadence. When you spend Movement during your Spotlight, you may take a willing Ally in Melee Range with you. When your Movement ends, place that Ally in Melee Range of you.

**2. Carbon Sting (Free)**
Cadence. After connecting with an Attack against an Enemy, spend all your remaining Movement to push that Enemy a number of tiles equal to the Movement spent in a straight line. If that push defeats that Enemy, gain +5 Movement this Spotlight and +5 AV on your next Attack.

**3. Hex Drift (Free)**
Cadence. After moving at least 6 tiles while remaining in Short Range of an Enemy, gain +10 AV on your next Attack against that Enemy this Player Phase. Choose one Enemy if multiple apply.

**4. Slipstream (Free)**
Passive. Gain +5 Movement this Player Phase when moving through Melee Range of an Enemy, up to +15.

**Special: The Pretender (Free)**
Exhaust. Swift. Until the start of the next Player Phase, add your current Max Movement to your DDV.
