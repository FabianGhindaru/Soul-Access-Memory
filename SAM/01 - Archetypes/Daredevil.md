## Daredevil
_"For those who believe that slowing down feels like dying."_

The Daredevil is velocity incarnate. Leaping from rooftop to rooftop, they refuse to be held still. Fueled by adrenaline and a total disregard for consequences, the Daredevil believes that they are the only truly free soul in the city.
Yet one question remains: is this freedom authentic, or simply a way to outrun their own insecurities? Time will see if moving faster than their demons is a viable choice.
_[Free & Reckless]_  AND  _[Skirmisher / Striker]_


### Innate Characteristics

**Asphalt Scars**
At the start of combat, you may voluntarily reduce your starting HP to 5. If you do, double your Max Movement for the remainder of the encounter. This choice is available only at combat start, and its effect reverts only when you are safe.


**Blink And I’m Gone**
Once per session, arrive somewhere that shouldn't be reachable such as a sealed floor, a moving vehicle, or collapsing structure. No roll or explanation required.



### Archetype Keystone

**No Time To Think**
Passive. All Daredevil’s combat abilities are Free.
Applies as a Sub-Archetype as well.



### Combat Abilities

**1. Joyride (Free)**
Cadence. When you spend Movement during your Spotlight, you may take a willing Ally in Melee Range with you without any downsides. When your movement ends, place them in a tile adjacent to you.

**2. Carbon Sting (Free)**
Cadence. Successfully hitting an Enemy allows you to spend all your current remaining Movement to push them a number of tiles equal to the Movement spent in a straight line. Defeating an Enemy with this collision damage grants +5 Movement for the current Spotlight and a +5 AV to your next attack.

**3. Hex Drift (Free)**
Cadence. After moving at least 6 tiles while remaining in Short Range of an Enemy, gain a +10 bonus to your next Attack Value against them this Player Phase. For multiple Enemies, choose only one.

**4. Slipstream (Free)**
Passive. Passing by an Enemy in Melee Range gives you +5 Movement this Player Phase, up to a maximum of +15.

**Special: The Pretender (Free)** 
Exhaust. Swift. Until the start of the next Player Phase, add your current Max Movement as a flat bonus to your Dodge Defense Value.
