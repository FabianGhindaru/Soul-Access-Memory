
## Professional
_"For those who know that warmth is a service, not a promise."_

The Professional doesn't split into a work self and a home self. They're the same person at 3 a.m. in a parking garage as they are across the table in good light. They notice the details no one asked for. They clean up even when no one will know. The charm is real, but it's a tool. The heart underneath runs cold and they've made peace with that. The job ends. The standard doesn't.

In combat, the Professional opens with Somewhere To Start on a target they can realistically defeat, then converts the kill through Suppression Chain and Efficient Spread into status and momentum against the next group. Task Switch keeps the right weapon in hand for each range, Insurance taxes status-marked enemies out of their next Enemy Phase, and End Shift closes the show by firing the entire reserve the moment an enemy falls.

**Fantasy:** Charming & Exacting
**Combat Style:** Striker / Versatile


### Innate Characteristics

**Suppression Chain**
Passive. After defeating an Enemy, treat one die in your next Attack Roll as its natural maximum (count it as a Crit).

**Smooth Operator**
Gain the Platinum Watch. Once per scene, hide a small object inside the Watch. It becomes completely invisible to all searches, allowing you to smuggle contraband through any security checkpoint.



### Archetype Keystone

**Meticulous Reserve**
Passive. Gain 3 additional Inventory Slots holding only weapons. Draw a weapon from those Slots without spending Beats.

As a Sub-Archetype, gain 1 additional Slot instead, and drawing from that Slot costs 1 Beat.


### Combat Abilities

**1. Somewhere To Start (Free)**
Exhaust. Declare before your first Attack of this combat. Gain +10 AV on that Attack.

**2. Task Switch (Free)**
Cadence. Swap your active weapon with one from your Meticulous Reserve. Gain +5 AV on your next Attack with that weapon this Spotlight. Gain +10 AV instead at Level 6.

**3. Insurance (1 Beat)**
Target an Enemy within Short Range with a Status Effect. That Enemy loses 1 Beat during its next Enemy Phase. This ability cannot affect the same Enemy more than once per combat.

**4. Efficient Spread (Free)**
Cadence. Make an Attack against an Enemy in range. If that Attack defeats that Enemy, apply Drain to each Enemy within Short Range of that Enemy if that Attack used a Melee or Ranged weapon. Apply Twist to each Enemy within Short Range of that Enemy if that Attack used a Manifestation.

**Special: End Shift (1 Beat)**
Exhaust. Combative. After defeating an Enemy, attack once with each weapon in your Inventory (includes Meticulous Reserve) for Free. This is an exception to the Commitment rule and may target the same Enemy multiple times. For each weapon you cannot use (e.g. no Enemy in range), gain 2 Movement instead.
