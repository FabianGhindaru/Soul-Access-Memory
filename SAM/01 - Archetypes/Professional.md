### Professional
_"For those who know that warmth is a service, not a promise."_

The Professional doesn't split into a work self and a home self. They're the same person at 3 a.m. in a parking garage as they are across the table in good light. They notice the details no one asked for. They clean up even when no one will know. The charm is real, but it's a tool. The heart underneath runs cold and they've made peace with that. The job ends. The standard doesn't.

_[Charming & Exacting]_


----------- CORE PASSIVES -----------------

**Suppression Chain**
Passive. Defeating an Enemy primes the next Attack Roll to automatically trigger a Crit.

**Smooth Operator**
Passive. Checks related to lies or manipulation start with with 1 Hit.
Gain the Platinum Watch. Once per scene, hide a small object inside the Watch. It becomes completely invisible to all searches, allowing you to smuggle contraband through any security checkpoint.



----------- ARCHETYPE KEYSTONE ---------------

**Meticulous Reserve**
Passive. Gain 3 additional Inventory Slots usable only for weapons. Drawing any weapon from these slots is Free.

As a Sub-Archetype you gain 1 additional Inventory Slot instead and the cost remains 2 Beats.


----------- COMBAT ABILITIES -----------------

**Somewhere To Start (Free)**
Exhaust. First Attack Value this combat gains a +10 bonus. Can only be used during your first turn.

**Task Switch (Free)**
Cadence. Switch your active weapon with one from your Meticulous Reserve. Grant your next Attack Value this turn with the new weapon a +5 bonus. Increases to +10 from Level 6.

**Insurance (2 Beats)**
Reduce the available Beats of an Enemy within Short Range by 2 until the end of your next turn if that Being has a Status Effect. This ability cannot affect the same Enemy more than once.

**Efficient Spread (2 Beats)**
Attack a Being. If this attack defeats an Enemy, apply Drain to all Enemies within Short Range when a Melee or Ranged Weapon was used, or apply Twist when a Manifestation was used instead.

**Special: End Shift (2 Beats)** 
Exhaust. Combative. Usable after defeating an Enemy. Attack with each weapon in your complete loadout once for Free. For each weapon that cannot be used (e.g., due to range), gain 2 Movement instead.
