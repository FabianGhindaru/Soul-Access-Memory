## Renegade
_"For those who don’t believe in the lie of peace."_

The Renegade is a legend first, flesh second. They carve through the city’s underground, leaving a wake of scorched exits and weaponized rumor. They know exactly what they are building.: a violently guarded autonomy.
The underworld is paralyzed, unsure whether to crown them or bury them. To the Renegade, that hesitation is exactly the space they need to operate entirely on their own terms.

In combat, the Renegade enters the stance that answers the current round and spends its Switch before changing, so every transition pays. Desperado and Outlaw punish grouped enemies, Rogue and Marauder thrive in close pressure, and Scoundrel answers status while feeding Drain into the next attack. Cycle through every equipped stance to refresh Devil's Roulette, then roll it when the crew can survive the worst outcomes and exploit the best.

**Fantasy:** Wild & Autonomous
**Combat Style:** Skirmisher / Versatile


### Innate Characteristics

**Crowded Mind**
Passive. Start with three combat abilities (Stances) instead of one. Each time you have entered all your equipped Stances, refresh Devil’s Roulette.

**Dark Alley Fame**
Once per session, your fans can start a fight with local authorities, rival syndicates, or any other Enemy group. If used in combat, they deal LV * Bounty (max. 40) Melee Damage to all Enemies in Long Range.


### Archetype Keystone

**Rebel Spirit (Free)**
Cadence. Change your current Stance to a different one. Each Stance has an Entry when you adopt it and a Switch when you leave it. Entry and Switch trigger automatically but apply only to this Spotlight.
Sub-Archetype: Gain 2 Stances instead.


### Combat Abilities

**Stance: Desperado**
Entry: Your next Attack may target one additional Being in range.
Switch: Gain +5 AV on your next Attack.

**Stance: Maverick**
Entry: Increase the range of your next Attack by one step (e.g. Short to Medium).
Switch: All Enemies in Short Range lose 5 Movement.

**Stance: Outlaw**
Entry: Your next Attack connecting with that target deals Entropy Damage to each other Being within Melee Range of that target (Max. 5).
Switch: Double your next Attack's CF for chaining.

**Stance: Rogue**
Entry: Gain +3 Movement for each Enemy in Melee Range (Max. +9 Movement).
Switch: If you defeated an Enemy this Spotlight, gain +5 AV to your next attack until the next Player Phase.

**Stance: Scoundrel**
Entry: Remove one Status Effect from yourself.
Switch: Apply Drain with your next connecting Attack.

**Stance: Marauder**
Entry: Heal yourself equal to Damage dealt by your next connecting Attack.
Switch: Gain +5 DV (max. +10) next time you are attacked for each Enemy in Melee Range.
