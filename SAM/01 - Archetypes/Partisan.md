## Partisan

_"For those who believe that trust is worth bleeding for."_

The Partisan is careful. They are aware of how dangerous the world is, always on the lookout for the threat, the double-cross, the other shoe about to drop. But when they choose an anchor, a crew, a cause, or a single person, they invest themselves with a terrifying intensity. Their loyalty is not based on blind faith, but on a fierce, counter-phobic refusal to let a hostile world take the one thing they have decided to care about. Where others panic at the first sign of an ambush, the Partisan just draws their weapon, because they knew it was coming all along.

In combat, the Partisan reads enemy grouping before committing a serum, since only one applies per Round. Dawn and Aurora recover the wounded, Noon and Twilight punish clusters, Midnight and Dusk blunt incoming pressure, and Eventide buys emergency repositioning. Empower the serums that answer the most common threat, and spend Witching Hour late, when refreshing every Exhausted serum at once rebuilds the whole engine for the final push.

**Fantasy:** Steadfast & Resolute
**Combat Style:** Guardian / Versatile


### Innate Characteristics


**Fear of Separation (Free)**
Exhaust. Until the end of the next Player Phase, any healing you provide to Fading Allies has Overflow. While this ability is active, you cannot be healed by any source. If you start Fading, this ability ends early.


**Preemptive Suspicion**
Once per session, gain direct insight into the possible dangers of the immediate future, such as ambushes, property damage, or possible betrayals.


### Archetype Keystone

**Golden Hours**
You have access to all the Serums listed below. Choose 3 Serums to use as your active combat abilities. You can freely swap your active Serums for any other Serum whenever you are out of combat.
At Levels 1, 3, and 6, you empower one of your Serums instead of gaining a new ability.
Serums are applied for Free to a Damage source (such as a weapon or Manifestation), and their effects trigger when that source connects with a Being. Apply only 1 Serum per Round. Applying a Serum to yourself requires no Damage source but costs 1 Beat. An applied but unused Serum dissipates on Spotlight handoff or Phase change.
As a Sub-Archetype, choose 1 already-empowered Serum instead, which becomes permanent.


### Combat Abilities

**1. Dawn Serum**
Harmless. Heal a Being for 2d4. Exhaust this Serum to heal that Being for 10 instead.
Empower: That healing has Overflow.

**2. Noon Serum**
Exhaust. Apply Burst.
Empower: Apply Drain, Burst, or Twist instead.

**3. Dusk Serum**
Exhaust. Harmless. Apply Resistance to Entropy Damage to an Ally until the start of the next Player Phase.
Empower: Apply Immunity instead.

**4. Twilight Serum**
Exhaust. Create a Short Range “Noxious Cloud” that deals 10 Entropy Damage to each Enemy inside that Cloud at the start of the Enemy Phase. Remove that Cloud at the end of the next Player Phase. Center that Cloud on the Being hit by that Damage source.
Empower: Deal 15 Entropy Damage instead.

**5. Midnight Serum**
Exhaust. Harmless. Create a Short Range “Ventablack Mist” that grants +10 to the Defense Values of each Being inside that Mist. Remove that Mist at the start of the next Player Phase. Center that Mist on the Being hit by that Damage source.
Empower: Grant +15 to those Defense Values instead.

**6. Aurora Serum**
Harmless. Apply the “Shimmering” Status Effect to a Being. When Shimmering PROCs, resolve an effect based on the target: if an Ally, heal the target for 10 and all other Allies in Short Range for 5; if an Enemy, deal 10 Entropy damage to the target and 5 Entropy damage to all other Enemies in Short Range. Shimmering does not expire at the end of the Round.
Empower: Increase the primary effect to 15 and the splash effect to 10 for both Allies and Enemies.

**7. Eventide Serum**
Exhaust. Harmless. Gain 10 Movement this Spotlight.
Empower: Gain 1 Beat and 15 Movement this Spotlight instead.

**Special: Witching Hour (1 Beat)**
End your Spotlight and lose all Beats. Refresh all your Exhausted Serums.
