### Dissident
_“For those who believe that their ideas are more precious than gold.”_

An agitator, underground organizer, or digital saboteur who weaponizes the beliefs of others. The Dissident turns crowds into weapons, turns enemies against each other, and sows chaos that the powerful cannot contain. Their power consists of ideas that spread faster than any bullet.


----------- CORE PASSIVES -----------------

**Stand Your Ground**
Passive. Gain immunity to Status Effects. PROC damage and effects still apply.

**Vox Populi**
Passive. The city's forgotten trust you inherently, offering shelter, basic supplies, and street-level intel for free.
Once per session, weaponize a crowd. You instantly spark a small riot, protest, or strike that locks down a small city region, halts local operations, or provides a distraction.



----------- ARCHETYPE KEYSTONE ---------------

**Enter the Source (2 Beats)**  
Exhaust. Combative. Swift. Enter a state of pure focus. While active, your Dissident combat abilities are automatically upgraded with their *Source* effects. This mode stops at the end of your next turn, or immediately if you start Fading.



----------- COMBAT ABILITIES -----------------

**Ignition (1 Beat)**  
Cadence. Swift. Trigger the PROC effect of a Status Effect currently on a Being in Medium Range instantly.  
*Source:* Apply the base version of that same Status Effect to another Being within Short Range of the target.

**Manifesto (1 Beat)**
Exhaust. Deal 10 Entropy damage to all Enemies in Short Range. Reduce the Movement of any Enemy damaged this way by 10 until the end of their next turn.
*Source:* Reduce the Movement by 20 instead.

**King’s Decree (4 Beats)**
Exhaust. Prevent the use of all abilities and Manifestations from an Enemy in Short Range until the end of your next turn.
*Source:* Affect all Enemies in Short Range instead.

**Radicalize (4 Beats)**  
Exhaust. Compel an Enemy in Short Range to perform an Attack against another Enemy of your choice within their range.
*Source:* Treat this attack as if it rolled a Critical Hit.

**Special: //Revolution (Free)** 
Exhaust. Apply 🩸Drain to all Enemies in Medium Range. Trigger all Status Effects currently active on all Enemies in Medium Range into their PROC effects instantly.
