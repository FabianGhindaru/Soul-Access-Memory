## Dissident

_"For those who believe that their ideas are more precious than gold."_

An agitator, underground organizer, or digital saboteur who weaponizes the beliefs of others. The Dissident turns crowds into weapons, turns enemies against each other, and sows chaos that the powerful cannot contain. Their power consists of ideas that spread faster than any bullet.

In combat, the Dissident seeds a single status early and detonates it with Ignition, spreading the base effect to a nearby enemy. Manifesto slows grouped enemies while damaging them, King's Decree silences the most dangerous one, and Radicalize turns an enemy's own attack against its allies. Revolution waits for the round when statuses are widest, triggering every PROC at once across Medium Range.

**Fantasy:** Idealistic & Relentless
**Combat Style:** Controller / Striker


### Innate Characteristics

**Stand Your Ground**
Passive. Gain Immunity to the standard effects of Status Effects. Status Effects can still be applied to you, and applying a second stack to you still PROCs as normal.

**Disillusionment Reveal**
Once per session, put one message on every screen, wall, and whisper network in a city region. Choose what it makes people do: gather, disperse, look the other way, or remember. It cannot be traced to you.


### Archetype Keystone

**Enter the Source**
Exhaust. Swift. Activate this mode for Free. While active, your combat abilities automatically gain their *Source* effects. The mode ends at the start of the next Player Phase, or immediately if you start Fading.
As a Sub-Archetype, choose 1 combat ability instead; its Source effect becomes permanent.


### Combat Abilities

**1. Ignition (1 Beat)**
Cadence. Swift. Choose a Being within Medium Range suffering from a Status Effect. Trigger that Status Effect's PROC as if a second stack was applied, then remove that Status Effect. You count as the attacker for that PROC.
*Source:* After that PROC resolves, apply the base version of that same Status Effect to another Being within Short Range of that original target.


**2. Manifesto (1 Beat)**
Exhaust. Deal 10 Entropy Damage to all Enemies within Short Range. Each Enemy damaged this way loses 10 Movement until the end of the next Enemy Phase.
*Source:* The Movement penalty is -20 instead.

**3. King's Decree (2 Beats)**
Exhaust. Choose an Enemy within Short Range. Until the end of the next Player Phase, that Enemy cannot use abilities, Manifestations, Techniques, or Swift reactions, but that Enemy may still move, make basic Attacks, and defend normally.
*Source:* Affect all Enemies within Short Range instead.


**4. Radicalize (2 Beats)**
Exhaust. Compel an Enemy within Short Range to immediately attack another Enemy of your choice within that Enemy's range. That attack does not consume that Enemy's Beats.
*Source:* The compelled attack counts as a Critical Hit on that Enemy's highest die type.


**Special: //Revolution (2 Beats)**
Exhaust. Apply Drain to all Enemies within Medium Range. Then trigger every Status Effect's PROC on those Enemies (including the Drain just applied). You count as the attacker for each of those PROCs.
