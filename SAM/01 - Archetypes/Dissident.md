### Dissident
_"For those who believe that their ideas are more precious than gold."_

An agitator, underground organizer, or digital saboteur who weaponizes the beliefs of others. The Dissident turns crowds into weapons, turns enemies against each other, and sows chaos that the powerful cannot contain. Their power consists of ideas that spread faster than any bullet.
_[Idealistic & Relentless]_ AND _[Controller / Saboteur]_



----------- [ INNATE CHARACTERISTICS ] -----------------

**Stand Your Ground**
Passive. Gain immunity to the standard effects of Status Effects. Status Effects can still be applied to you, and applying a second stack to you still PROCs as normal.

**Vox Populi**
Once per session, weaponize a crowd. You instantly spark a small riot, protest, or strike that locks down a small city region, halts local operations, or provides a distraction.



----------- [ ARCHETYPE KEYSTONE ] ---------------

**Enter the Source**
Exhaust. Swift. Activate this mode for Free. While active, your Dissident combat abilities automatically gain their *Source* effects. The mode ends at the start of the next Player Phase, or immediately if you start Fading.
As a Sub-Archetype, choose 1 combat ability instead; its Source effect becomes permanent.



----------- [ COMBAT ABILITIES ] -----------------

**1. Ignition (1 Beat)**
Cadence. Swift. Choose a Being within Medium Range of you suffering from a Status Effect. That Status Effect immediately PROCs as if a second stack had been applied, then is removed. You count as the attacker for this PROC.
*Source:* After the PROC resolves, apply the base version of that same Status Effect to another Being within Short Range of the original target.

**2. Manifesto (1 Beat)**
Exhaust. Deal 10 Entropy damage to all Enemies within Short Range. Each Enemy damaged this way suffers -10 Movement until the end of the next Enemy Phase.
*Source:* The Movement penalty is -20 instead.

**3. King's Decree (2 Beats)**
Exhaust. Choose an Enemy within Short Range. Until the end of the next Player Phase, that Enemy cannot use abilities, Manifestations, Techniques, or Swift reactions, but it may still move, make basic attacks, and defend normally.
*Source:* Affect all Enemies within Short Range instead.

**4. Radicalize (2 Beats)**
Exhaust. Compel an Enemy within Short Range of you to immediately attack another Enemy of your choice within the compelled Enemy's range. This does not consume the compelled Enemy's Beats.
*Source:* The compelled attack counts as a Critical Hit on the highest dice type of their attack.

**Special: //Revolution (2 Beats)**
Exhaust. Apply Drain to all Enemies within Medium Range. Then, every Status Effect on those Enemies (including the Drain just applied) instantly PROCs. You count as the attacker for all of these PROCs.
