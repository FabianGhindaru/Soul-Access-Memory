
### Firestarter (Tier I) 1HB ◐
**[ 1d4 (CF 1) Arcane ]**
Combative. Manifest a spark that applies 🔥Burst.
_"The warmth is part of human nature."_

// Presentation Ideas
•     Flick a cigarette at them, exploding when it makes contact.
•     A music note appears above their head. A loud pop hurts their ears.
•     A small, playful ember dances around them before detonating.

### Thornburst (Tier I) 1HB ◐
Combative. Harmless. Manifest Crystalline Thorns around you. The first Being that lands a Melee attack on you takes 5 Entropy damage and gains 🔥Burst. Ends at the start of your next turn or after the attack.
Amplify: No additional effect.
_"I have to protect my Heart somehow..."_

// Presentation Ideas
•     Jagged rings of static electricity orbit your body, snapping at anyone who gets close.
•     Your clothes momentarily turn into sharpened obsidian glass when struck.
•     A halo of floating, ethereal rose stems coils defensively around your shoulders.

---

### Eternity Fantasy (Tier I) 1HB ◐
Utility. Create a physical object displaying the last words spoken by a deceased Being. 
Amplify: No additional effect.
_"You will only die when these words are forgotten."_

// Presentation Ideas
•     A delicate origami crane folds itself from thin air, inscribed with their final breath.
•     A vinyl record forms in your hand, infinitely looping their final spoken sentence.
•     A glowing, ethereal locket opens to project the words in hovering, neon text.

### Force Projection (Tier I) 1HB ◐
**[ 1d4 (CF 1) Arcane ]**
Harmless. Combative. Push a Being up to 5 tiles in a chosen direction.
Amplify: Gain +5 additional tiles to the push distance in addition to the die increase.
_"A gentle push with the force of a falling mountain."_

// Presentation Ideas
•     A ghostly, oversized hand materializes to simply swat the target away.
•     You mimic blowing a kiss, which strikes them like a cannonball.
•     A sudden, localized hurricane-force gust of wind blasts from your palm.

### Phantasmagoria (Tier I) 1HB ◐
**[ 1d6 (CF 2) Arcane ]**
Combative. Manifest a Shadow to perform one action: either a physical interaction (move an object, pull a lever, throw an item) OR Attack a Being in Short Range. 
Amplify: Add and link one additional action.
_"Shadows dance when they are not seen."_

// Presentation Ideas
•     Your own silhouette detaches, runs over, punches them, and snaps back.
•     A shadowy tendril slithers out from under a door to pull the lever.
•     An inky, humanoid figure forms from spilled coffee to lob a mug at their head.

---

### Phantom Reach (Tier I) 1HB ◐
Combative. Increase the Range of your Melee Weapon to Short Range until the end of your next turn. 
Amplify: Increase its Range by an additional step (e.g., Short to Medium).
_"The boundary is thinner than the edge of your blade."_

// Presentation Ideas
•     Your weapon's shadow detaches and stretches across the floor to cut them.
•     You slash the air, sending a compressed vacuum blade flying forward.
•     A spectral extension of your arm and weapon mirrors your exact swing from a distance.

### Synesthesia (Tier I) 1HB ◐
Utility. Manifest a visual, auditory, olfactory, tactile, or gustatory illusion of a known sensation upon a Being or object. The effect lasts until the end of the scene or until dismissed. Win a Soul Clash to convince a Being that the illusion is real. 
Amplify: No additional effect.
_"…would describe it as a Kaleidoscope of Experiences."_

// Presentation Ideas
•     They suddenly taste pennies and blood, distracting them from reality.
•     A phantom swarm of buzzing bees surrounds their ears, entirely invisible.
•     The overwhelming scent of blooming jasmine completely masks everything else in the room.

---

### With A Thunderous Applause (Tier I) 1HB ◐
Utility. Harmless. Manifest a Column of Light centered on a point in Short Range that lasts until the end of the scene. Invisible or concealed Beings inside the column are immediately revealed.
Amplify: No additional effect.
_"...revealing what lies hidden."_

// Presentation Ideas
•     A theatrical spotlight drops from the sky, accompanied by a faint drumroll.
•     A swarm of glowing fireflies explodes outward, sticking to and outlining anything invisible.
•     A massive, spectral camera flash strobes, burning hidden figures into the afterimage of the room.
