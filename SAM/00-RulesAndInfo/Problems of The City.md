
### 1. The Algiere Family (The Traditional Syndicate)
	The Algiere aren’t just a single organization. They’re a coalition of smaller, blood-bound crime families held together by a rigid old-world code of loyalty, fear, and debt. Their power reaches through the Bloom’s entertainment halls and the Roots’ black markets alike. They value tradition, ritual, and brutal clarity. When they come for you, they come like a pack.

* **Associato (The Punks):** Street-level wannabes clawing for a spot in a made family. Most are non-Intrinsic (they weren’t born with Manifestations) so they turn to illegal, black-market Handler mods to replicate basic abilities. They’ll rush you in reckless swarms, but fold the moment the tide turns.

* **Soldato (The Foot Soldiers):** Made men and women, blood-sworn and dangerous. Their ancestral Manifestations run to brute force: skin that hardens like stone, fists that ignite on impact, etc. They carry standard ballistic weapons and fight with cold, coordinated sweeps.

* **Bastone (“The Stick” / The Muscle):** Bastone enforcers are wheeled in only when a message needs to be sent or a stalemate broken. Massively augmented, terrifyingly indifferent to injury, they don’t bother with firearms. They rip pipes from walls, swing heavy machinery, and use Manifestations that obliterate cover.

* **Executore (The Assassins):** Quiet problem-solvers. Their Manifestations bend perception (light warps around them, sound dies in their vicinity) . They attack from blind angles with poisoned blades or suppressed rifles. 

* **Consigliere (The Memory Weavers):** The true psychological threat. Consiglieri rarely draw a weapon. They can conjure hallucinations drawn from your own buried past, force traumatic flashbacks that freeze you mid-step, or temporarily strip your muscle memory: your hands forget how to hold a gun, how to form a fist. 

* **Capo-Famiglia (Family Bosses):**  A Capo projects an aura of overwhelming dread that makes enemies hesitate, or a warped sense of family that drives their people into a fearless, unbreakable frenzy. So long as a boss is breathing, their crew fights like a single, desperate organism.

### 2. Machina (The Info-Broker Syndicate)
Machina operatives don’t want a fair fight. They want a fight that was already lost before you showed up. Hackers, blackmailers, and voyeurs, they treat the Handler network as a weapon. They already know your worst moments. 

* **Scrapers:** Low-level data thieves, always in numbers. They blanket an area with signal jammers and EMP grenades. They don’t kill you directly, they just make you easier to kill.

* **Parsers:** Psychological warfare specialists. They pull from countless stolen memory-archives and broadcast directly into your earpiece: a loved one’s voice begging for help, a shameful secret you’ve never spoken aloud, a perfectly simulated recreation of your worst mistake.

* **Archivists:** Walking, armored server farms whose brains hold priceless, illegal memory-troves. Because their cargo is more valuable than their lives, Archivists are chromed into near-tanks. Their minds run predictive combat algorithms that make them maddeningly evasive, and they constantly deploy swarms of autonomous drones that act as eyes, shields, and blades.

* **Admins (Elite Brokers):** Admin operatives are hooked so deeply into the city grid that the infrastructure bends to their will. They fight you with the district you’re standing in.

### 3. The Gilded Jacks (The Theatrical Thieves)
The Jacks are showmen, illusionists, and adrenaline purists. To them, a crime is a performance and the audience must be stunned. Every encounter with them feels like being trapped inside a lethal, high-speed magic show.

* **Clubs (The Muscle/Bouncers):** Brutality wrapped in impeccable tailoring. Clubs carry heavy weapons. Their violence is blunt, inevitable, but somehow still elegant.

* **Spades (The Infiltrators):** They attack from the dark. They treat a battle like a canvass they’re cutting into pieces.

* **Diamonds (The Distractions):**  A Diamond becomes a gallery of mirrors. Five, six, a dozen copies, all moving independently. They want you to empty a magazine on a reflection while the real one snaps precise laser bolts into your side. 

* **Hearts (The Empaths):** The heart that pumps the Jacks’ metaphorical blood. They can flood your system with unnatural rage until you swing at whoever’s nearest, or pull life from the fallen and pour it back into a grinning ally. The sound they make when they do it is somewhere between a sigh and a song.

* **Jokers (Wildcards):** No plan survives a Joker.

### 5. Daedalus Dynamics [DaeMics] (The Corporate Entity)
DaeMics views violence as just another product-research stream: a “live-fire data-gathering event.” There is a sterile, clinical horror to them. Their people are either assets or liabilities. Eevery encounter is a harvest of combat metrics, extracted coldly, logged without emotion.

* **Hazmats (Test Subjects):** Root-bottom citizens whose debts were purchased and their bodies strapped into unstable prototype exosuits. They carry experimental weapons still hissing with imperfections. They hit terrifyingly hard, but the hardware easily breaks.

* **Lab Techs (Data Gatherers):** Unarmoured figures standing at the back, datapads glowing. They don’t flinch. As you fight, they remotely trigger experimental shields, inject combat stimulants directly into the Hazmats’ bloodstream, or vent an airborne cocktail of nerve agents into enclosed spaces.

* **Sanitizers (Clean-up Crews):** Identical, featureless white armor, blank faceplates, absolute silence. No comms chatter, no battlecries, no fear. Their weapons dissolve matter on a molecular level: designed to leave no body, no trace, no evidence that a firefight ever took place. They are immune to any psychological tactic; there’s nothing in there to reach.

* **The Proxies:** Autonomous bipedal mechs with no soul and a distributed consciousness. Destroy one, and its final milliseconds of sensor data are uploaded to every other unit in the network. The surviving machines adapt, their armour re-tuning itself against whatever weapon just killed their twin.

### 6. Mantra (The Cybernetic Corp)
Mantra operates like a polished paramilitary force that also works as its own best advertisement. They sell the finest Handler-integrated chrome, and their operatives are walking catalogues. Squads deploy in tight five-person configurations designated by Greek letters (Epsilon, Gamma, Theta, etc.) precision teams that function like clockwork.

* **The Commander:** The tactical brain. The Commander’s upgraded Handler paints threat markers directly onto your silhouette, feeding a continuous stream of positional and predictive data to every Mantra operative in the squad. As long as Alpha is standing, the whole unit moves with surgical focus.

* **The Gunner:** Walking suppression. Shoulder-mounted smart weapons scan independently; recoil-dampening arms lock onto firing lanes. If you’re caught in that zone, you exist only behind cover or briefly outside of it.

* **The Sniper:** Optical camo cloaks bleed them into the environment, while experimental railguns punch through walls, furniture, and the assumption of safety. The Sniper constantly relocates after every shot. Hunters have to track the residual ionization trail of their weapon, or rely on thermals, sound, and sheer paranoid instinct.

* **The Manifester:** A marriage of magic and machine that rarely looks comfortable. The Manifester operatives push their ancestral gifts to apex levels through brutal cybernetic amplification. The power is undeniable and the physical agony on their face is just as real.

* **The Medic:** The squad’s support spine. A Medic carries compact fabrication systems capable of repairing shattered chrome, reviving a downed operative, or deploying autonomous turrets in seconds. Any tactical sense says The Medic dies first.
