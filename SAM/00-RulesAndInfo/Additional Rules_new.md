
# Additional Rules
Rules and systems not covered in the Core Rules (Light). Use these at your table as needed.

---

## 1. Movement & Terrain

### Jumping
You can jump for Free (no Beat cost) a distance equal to your Body dice pool:

| Body | Dice Pool | Jump Range |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| 0–4 | 1d6 | Short (≤3 tiles) |
| 5–8 | 2d6 | Medium (≤6 tiles) |
| 9–12 | 3d6 | Long (≤9 tiles) |

To jump farther, make a **Body check** (2 Beats). The number of Hits required equals the additional range bands beyond your base: 1 Hit for one band farther, 2 Hits for two bands farther, etc.

> **Example:** Body 6 (2d6 → Medium base). Jump Long requires 1 Hit. Jump Very Long requires 2 Hits. Body 3 (1d6 → Short base) jumping Long requires 2 Hits.

### Climbing
Climbing uses the same rules as jumping. Your base climbing distance per turn equals your Body dice range (Short/Medium/Long). Surfaces beyond your base range band require a Body check with Hits equal to the additional bands.

> **Note:** Both Jumping and Climbing still use the respective Movement (e.g., jumping in long range uses up to 9 Movement). 


### Difficult Terrain
When the Conductor designates an area as difficult (rubble, deep mud, waist-high water), each tile costs 2 Movement instead of 1. 

### Grid Types
- **Hex grids:** 6 adjacent tiles.
- **Square grids:** 8 adjacent tiles (orthogonal and diagonal).

## 2. Stealth

### Hiding
To attempt stealth, make a **Grit check** (Body + Mind) against the highest Grit among observing Enemies. Success = you are Hidden from all Enemies.

- Hidden beings cannot be directly targeted by single-target attacks.
- Attacking, casting a Manifestation, or moving into open space breaks Hidden.
- Hidden beings roll their first attack with **+1 automatic Hit** on the damage dice (treat one die as its maximum before rolling).


## 3. Physical Interactions

### Inventory Slots
You have **6 Inventory Slots** for gear.

- Weapons and reasonable items (medkit, tools, small gadgets) occupy **1 Slot**.
- Heavier equipment (armor plates, explosives, large devices) occupies **2 or 3 Slots**: the item's description tells you.
- Always-equipped Melee + Ranged weapons do not count toward your 6 Slots.

### Grappling (Chokehold / Human Shield)
To grapple a Being, make a **Body vs. Body** versus check. On success:

- The target's **Movement is 0**.
- Your Movement is halved while maintaining the hold (each tile costs 2 instead of 1), but the grappled Being follows you completely.
- The target may attempt to escape as a Major Action (versus check again).

**Body Shield:** If you take damage while grappling, and the final damage against you is **lower than your Body attribute**, the damage is redirected to the grappled being instead of you.

> **Example:** Body 4. AV 18 vs your MDV 15 = 3 damage. 3 < your Body 4 → **the grappled enemy takes the 3 damage**, not you. AV 18 vs MDV 12 = 6 damage. 6 ≥ Body 4 → you take it normally.

**Losing the hold:** The grapple ends if you lose a subsequent Body clash against the target, or if you take any damage that isn't redirected. Releasing the hold is a Free Action.

### Throwing Objects
You can throw an object a distance based on your Body dice pool:

| Body | Dice Pool | Throw Range |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| 0–4 | 1d6 | Short (≤3 tiles) |
| 5–8 | 2d6 | Medium (≤6 tiles) |
| 9–12 | 3d6 | Long (≤9 tiles) |

Heavy objects reduce this by one range band (e.g., Body 1d6 with a heavy object → Melee range only). Throwing a weapon counts as a Ranged attack using the weapon's profile. Throwing an improvised object (chair, rock, debris) deals your Body dice pool as Melee damage with no weapon bonus.

### Pushing & Dragging
You can push or drag a grappled target up to half your Movement (round down) as part of your move. Pushing or dragging a heavy object requires a **Body check** (1 Hit per round).

### Object Durability
When players attack objects (doors, barricades, machinery), use AV normally. Objects do not roll DV — they have a fixed Damage Threshold:

| Object                       | Threshold | HP  |
| ---------------------------- | :-------: | :-: |
| Wooden door, glass pane      |     0     | 10  |
| Reinforced door, metal grate |     5     | 20  |
| Vault door, structural wall  |    10     | 40  |

Damage below the threshold is ignored. Entropy and Aeon damage ignore thresholds.

## 6. Social

### Relationships
Throughout the campaign, you meet NPCs who become allies, rivals, or enemies. Track individual relationships when the Conductor calls for it.

| Relationship | Modifier | Effect |
|-------------|:--------:|--------|
| **Enemy** | −2 | Actively works against you. Hostile on sight. |
| **Rival** | −1 | Wants to outdo you. Competitive, not violent. |
| **Acquaintance** | 0 | Neutral. Knows your name, nothing owed. |
| **Friend** | +1 | Willing to help with minor favors. |
| **Confidant** | +2 | Deep trust. Will take risks for you. |

The relation ship modifier adds or removes Hits when interacting with that NPC (e.g., a check requiring 1 Hit against an Enemy, starts at -1 Hits, a check against a confidant starts at 2 Hits)

### Interrogation & Persuasion
When you attempt to extract information or sway an NPC, make a **Mind check** (persuasion) or a **Will check** (interrogation, deception). The difficulty depends on the NPC's relationship:

| Relationship | Difficulty |
|-------------|------------|
| Confidant | 1 Hit |
| Friend | 1 Hit |
| Acquaintance | 2 Hits |
| Rival | 2 Hits |
| Enemy | 3 Hits |

Apply the relationship modifier from the table above to your roll. Repeated persuasion attempts on the same NPC in the same scene increase difficulty by +1 Hit each time.

## 7. Recovery

You automatically heal to full HP and refresh all Exhausted abilities at the end of combat. There is no attrition between fights — each combat starts fresh.

Luck Dice refresh at the start of each new session.

Travel between districts is cinematic — the Conductor describes the journey in narrative time. No travel mechanics.

## 8. Environmental Rules (Downpour)

When rain falls in Noverra, the Conductor rolls once on the Downpour table at the start of the mission. The effect applies to **all combats** for the entire mission. Players know the result before committing to the gig.

| d12 | Effect |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | 1 Beat maximum per turn. |
| 2 | Cannot Chain attacks. |
| 3 | Max Movement halved. |
| 4 | Start each combat with Drain. |
| 5 | All Attack Values halved (round down). |
| 6 | All Defense Values halved (round down). |
| 7 | All CF values halved (round down). |
| 8 | Mitigation unavailable (includes Burn). |
| 9 | Dodge unavailable (includes Burn). |
| 10 | One weapon (your choice) has 0 CF. |
| 11 | No Weapon Values on Attack Values. |
| 12 | All Attack/Defense Values suffer −5 (minimum final value 1). |

> **Example:** Roll 5 ("AV halved"). Your typical melee AV of 22 becomes 11. Status Effect procs and Manifestations become critical — raw damage won't carry you.

## 9. Currency & Commerce

Noverra runs on two currencies.

### Syn-C
Digital credit tied to your Handler. Abundant. Accepted everywhere in the Bloom. **Entirely traceable.** Every transaction is logged.

### Kaph
Heavy, physical, pre-Collapse currency. Untraceable. Required for black market deals, underworld access, and surviving the underground. Kaph is the real money.

### Lifestyle Thresholds
Your total combined wealth determines what you can purchase without tracking individual costs.

| Tier | Wealth | The Effect |
|------|--------|------------|
| **Tight** | 0–249 | Track every expense. Even a 5 Syn-C meal is deducted. |
| **Stable** | 250–1,999 | Purchases of 25 or less are free. |
| **Flush** | 2,000+ | Purchases of 100 or less are free. |

Dropping into a lower tier removes the higher tier's perks immediately.

### The Rainform Rule
When the dome opens and the city enters **Rainform Mode**, the Handler enters SafeMode. **All Syn-C commerce suspends.** Only Kaph works. If you have 5,000 Syn-C but 0 Kaph when the storm hits, you are effectively broke. Plan ahead.

## 10. Additional Combat Rules

### Manifestation Crits
Manifestation attacks **cannot Crit**. No Explodes. No Lethality bonuses.

### Range Requirements for Chaining
Both attacks must have matching range, **or** Ranged at Point Blank (Melee Range) can chain with Melee.

## 12. Luck Dice
Your Soul attribute grants a pool of d6s usable each session.

| Soul | Luck Dice |
|------|:---------:|
| 0 | 0 |
| 1–4 | 1 |
| 5–8 | 2 |
| 9–12 | 3 |

Spend one Luck die to adjust the total result of any attack or check — increase or decrease by the rolled value. Only one Luck die per roll. Usable on yourself, allies, or enemies. Pool refreshes at the start of each session. 
Spending a Luck die is a Swift and Free action. 

> **Example:** Enemy AV 16 against your DDV 14 — you'd take full damage. Spend a Luck die, roll 3. Decrease their AV to 13. DDV 14 ≥ 13 → Dodge succeeds.
