# Infamy System

Infamy is how much the city wants you dead. It tracks your wanted level, attracts bounty hunters, and shapes how Noverra reacts to your presence. One number per Protagonist, range **0 to 5**.

---

## Generating Infamy

After a gig, check which of the following happened. Each Protagonist present gains **+1 Infamy** for each. **Maximum +3 per gig.**

| Action | Infamy |
|--------|:------:|
| Gunfire in public (multiple civilians alarmed) | +1 |
| Bodies left behind | +1 |
| Witnesses escaped | +1 |
| Heavy property damage (explosions, breached walls, structural collapse) | +1 |
| Civilians killed | +1 |
| A boss or named NPC was killed | +1 |
| Faction retaliation was publicly declared | +1 |
| Gig took place in same district as the last one | +1 |

Protagonists who weren't on the gig don't gain Infamy from it. Splitting the crew has consequences — fewer hands on the job, fewer faces on the posters.

**Starting Infamy:** All Protagonists start at **0**. The Renegade's **Dark Alley Fame** passive adds +1 at character creation — a Renegade always starts at 1.

## Infamy Effects

| Infamy | State | Prices | Social | Hunters | Intimidation |
|:------:|-------|:------:|--------|:-------:|:-----------:|
| 0–1 | Cold | Normal | Neutral | None | None |
| 2–3 | Warm | +25% | −1 Hit with authorities | 1-in-6 day | +1 Hit |
| 4–5 | Hot | +50%, Bloom off | −1 Hit with all non-crew | 2-in-6 + named | +2 Hits |

At Infamy 5, gig availability drops to 1 instead of 3. At Infamy 4+, all factions suffer −1 standing while Infamy remains at 4+ — you're bad for business.

## Increasing From Other Triggers

| Trigger | Infamy Change |
|---------|:-------------:|
| Faction at standing −3 posts a bounty | +1 |
| Named bounty hunter is defeated | +1 to all who fought |
| Protagonist is clearly identified during a loud gig but wasn't present for the whole thing | +1 |
| A Protagonist kills a faction boss | +2 |

## Decay

**Auto:** Infamy −1 after every completed gig. Keeping your head down helps.

**Active:**
- **Lay Low** (1 day action): Infamy −1.
- **Pay Bribes** (200 Kaph per point, max −2 per cycle): Data-scrubbers, Watch sergeants, Handler cleanup.
- **Do a Free Gig** for a faction at standing 0+: Infamy −2, faction standing +1. They clean your trail.
