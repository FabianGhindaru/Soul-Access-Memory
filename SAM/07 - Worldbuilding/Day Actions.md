# Day Actions

Between gigs, each Protagonist gets **2 day actions** per gig cycle. Use them or lose them. Protagonists don't need to stay together during the day.

---

## Action List

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Shop** | Buy or sell gear, weapons, armor, upgrades, or items at current Infamy-adjusted prices. |
| **Gather Intel** | Find information about an upcoming gig, a faction, an NPC, or a district. |
| **Call a Contact** | Reach out to an NPC with standing +1 or higher. Ask for a favor, information, or service. The NPC may help freely, ask for something in return, or refuse — depends on standing and the request. |
| **Lay Low** | Infamy −1. Multiple Protagonists can Lay Low in the same cycle (each spends 1 action, Infamy −1 per action). |
| **Recover** | Heal to full HP (free). Or, remove one Mortal Reminder — requires a Trauma Kit and a successful Grit check. |

---

## Freeform Days

If the table wants a narrative session with no gig, the Conductor declares a **freeform day**. No action limit. Players describe what they do and the Conductor responds. Shopping, socializing, and city exploration happen freely.

Infamy still decays by 1 at the end of a freeform day, provided the crew didn't draw attention. If someone does something loud during a freeform day, generate Infamy from the checklist as normal.

Freeform days are for:
- Resolving personal character arcs
- Meeting new NPCs organically
- Exploring districts for future gig opportunities
- Building relationships with factions outside the gig loop
