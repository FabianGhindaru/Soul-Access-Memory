# Factions & NPCs

---

## Part 1: Faction Standing (Crew)

Faction standing is tracked per crew, not per Protagonist. It represents the crew's collective reputation with the major powers of Noverra. Ranges **−3 to +3** per faction.

### The Scale

| Standing | State | Effect |
|:--------:|-------|--------|
| +3 | Beloved | Safehouse access. Free basic gear. Emergency extraction. Unique high-reward gigs from this faction. |
| +2 | Trusted | Faction-specific shops and intel. +1 Hit on social checks with faction members. Priority gig offers. |
| +1 | Friendly | Cooperative. Normal gigs. 10% discount at faction-affiliated shops. |
| 0 | Neutral | Standard prices and interactions. The faction has no opinion of you. |
| −1 | Suspicious | Members are uncooperative. Gig offers are riskier or lower-paying. −1 Hit on social checks. |
| −2 | Hostile | Shops refuse service. Members may report your location to bounty hunters. −2 Hits on social checks. Minor bounties posted against the crew. |
| −3 | Hunted | The faction actively wants you dead. Their enforcers attack on sight in their territory. Major bounty posted. Gig offers from this faction are traps. |

### Moving Standing

| Gig Result | Standing Change |
|-------------|:---------------:|
| Faction directly helped (completed a gig for them) | +1 |
| Faction's enemy was harmed | +1 |
| Faction directly harmed (killed members, stole from them, sabotaged) | −1 |
| Faction's boss was killed | −2 (and +1 to all rival factions) |
| Gig was clean — no one knows who did it | No change with harmed factions |

Floors and ceilings: standing cannot drop below −3 or rise above +3. Once at −3, a faction cannot be placated. Once at +3, a faction treats you as family.

### Standing and Gigs

The three gigs offered each cycle are influenced by standing:
- Factions at **+1 or higher** offer priority gigs (higher pay, better intel, faction backup).
- Factions at **0** offer standard gigs.
- Factions at **−1** offer conditional gigs (lower pay, stricter terms, no support).
- Factions at **−2 or lower** do not offer gigs. If one arrives through an intermediary, the Conductor rolls a d6 — on a 1, it's a trap.

## Part 2: NPCs

NPC relationships are per-Protagonist and tracked separately from faction standing. A Protagonist can be beloved by the Algiere (crew standing +3) while personally hated by a specific Associato (NPC standing −2).

### NPC Standing

Each Protagonist tracks their standing with significant NPCs using the same **−3 to +3** scale.

| Standing | State | Effect |
|:--------:|-------|--------|
| +3 | Devoted | The NPC would take a bullet for you. Share secrets freely. Offer sanctuary. |
| +2 | Trusted | The NPC shares useful intel. Will do favors without immediate return. |
| +1 | Friendly | Cooperative. Minor help if it costs them nothing. |
| 0 | Neutral | Transactional. Will help for payment or mutual benefit. |
| −1 | Cold | Avoids you. Won't help unless forced or paid double. |
| −2 | Hostile | Actively works against you when safe to do so. Warns your enemies. |
| −3 | Enemy | Seeks your ruin. Will risk themselves to harm you. |

NPC standing changes through story events, not a mechanical table. The Conductor moves it based on what happens in play.

### NPC Profiles

Every significant NPC has three tags. These are for the Conductor — players discover them through roleplay, intel gathering, or social checks.

| Tag | What It Is |
|-----|------------|
| **Want** | What the NPC desires. Knowing this tells you how to bribe or recruit them. |
| **Fear** | What the NPC is afraid of. Knowing this gives you leverage. |
| **Secret** | Something the NPC hides. Using this is powerful but dangerous — if they find out you know, the relationship may break permanently. |

**Example:** *"Mateo Rossi — Want: control of the Crownline transit. Fear: his son inheriting the family. Secret: he ordered the hit on his predecessor and framed the Machina."*

### Leverage

If you know an NPC's Want, Fear, or Secret, you have **leverage**. When you use leverage in a social scene:

- **Want:** Offer them what they desire. +1 automatic Hit on the relevant check. If you deliver it, +2 Hits.
- **Fear:** Threaten what they're afraid of. +1 automatic Hit. If they know you can follow through, +2 Hits. Using fear damages standing (−1 after the scene, whether you succeed or not).
- **Secret:** Blackmail or expose. +2 automatic Hits. If the NPC discovers you know their secret and you use it, standing drops to −2 permanently. Secrets burn bridges.

### Favors

NPCs can owe you favors, and you can owe them. A favor is a one-time promise.

**Earning a favor:** Do something significant for the NPC. The Conductor decides if it qualifies.

**Calling in a favor:** No roll required. The NPC delivers what they promised. After a favor is called, the relationship returns to neutral terms — they don't owe you anymore, and you don't owe them.

**Owing a favor:** An NPC can call in their favor at any time. Refusing to honor a favor you owe drops standing to −2 with that NPC.

### Social Clash

When persuasion is contested, it's a derived check. Two approaches:

- **Convince (Will):** Logic, reason, negotiation. Target opposes with Will.
- **Command (Aura):** Presence, intimidation, force of personality. Target opposes with Aura.

Momentum works normally: ties go to the defender. Leverage, standing modifier, and Infamy intimidation bonus all apply as relevant. A single scene typically resolves in 1–3 clashed rolls — enough for tension, not a minigame.

### Burning Bridges

A Protagonist can **burn** a relationship with an NPC to guarantee success on one social action related to that NPC. No roll — it just works. Afterward, standing drops to **−3 permanently.** The NPC becomes an Enemy. This cannot be undone.

Only usable on NPCs at standing +1 or higher. You can't burn a bridge that's already ash.
