# Armor Upgrades
Upgrades are installed into armor's available slots. Swapping upgrades between armor pieces is free outside combat. To install and use upgrades, you must have the corresponding Aptitude skill.

---

## Tier I

### Reinforced Plating
**Type:** Mitigate. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 300 Syn-C

Increase your Mitigation Defense Value (MDV) by +5.

> *"Heavy, but so is the alternative."*

---

### Lightweight Composite
**Type:** Dodge. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 300 Syn-C

Increase your Dodge Defense Value (DDV) by +5.

> *"Aerodynamics built for dangerous situations"*

---

### Trauma Padding
**Type:** Both. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 300 Syn-C

Increase your Max HP by +5 while this armor is equipped.

---

### Stealth Lining
**Type:** Both. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 300 Syn-C

Gain +1 automatic Hit on all Stealth checks. The armor's surface absorbs light and dampens sound.

> *"Matte-black nano-fiber coating."*

---

### Daedalus Kinetic Dampener
**Type:** Both. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 400 Syn-C

Forced movement against you (push, pull, grapple-drag) is reduced by 10 tiles.

> *"Micro-gyroscopic filaments resist lateral force." — Daedalus R&D Brief*

---

## Tier II

### Reactive Gel Layer
**Type:** Mitigate. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 600 Syn-C

Extend your Glancing Blow window from 5 to 10.
(When AV - DDV <= 10, you take half damage instead of full)


---

### Adrenal Weave
**Type:** Both. **Activation:** Free (O).
**Price:** 600 Syn-C

Cadence. Gain +10 additional Movement. At the end of your turn, take 5 Entropy damage.

---

### Impact Feedback
**Type:** Mitigate. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 600 Syn-C

After successfully Mitigating an attack, deal 5 Entropy damage back to the attacker. Once per round.

> *"Piezoelectric lattice."*

---

### Status Dampeners
**Type:** Both. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 600 Syn-C

Status Effects (Burst, Drain, Twist) deal half damage to you (rounded down). The PROC effects are also halved.

---

### Tissue Recalibrator
**Type:** Both. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 600 Syn-C

Ignore instances of damage that are 5 or lower.

---

### Daedalus Phase-Fiber
**Type:** Dodge. **Activation:** 1 Beat (Half).
**Price:** 700 Syn-C

Cadence. After a successful Dodge, gain Hidden. Hidden breaks on your next offensive action or if you move into open space without cover.

> *"Bends light around the wearer." — Daedalus R&D Brief*

---

## Tier III

### Mirage Coating
**Type:** Dodge. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 1,000 Syn-C

The first attack targeting you each round suffers -10 AV. 

---

### Entropy Sink
**Type:** Both. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 1,000 Syn-C

All Entropy damage you take is halved (rounded down). 

> *"Grounded dissipation mesh."*

---

### Emergency Override
**Type:** Both. **Activation:** Free (O).
**Price:** 1,000 Syn-C

Exhaust. Overflow. When Fading, instantly heal 15 HP.

> *"One more chance. Make it count."*

---

### Second Skin
**Type:** Both. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 1,000 Syn-C

At combat start choose one: gain +1 additional Mitigate, or +1 additional Dodge.

> *"The armor learns how you fight. It shifts its defensive profile to match the threat before you even recognize it."*

---

### Daedalus Null-Field Generator
**Type:** Both. **Activation:** 2 Beats.
**Price:** 1,100 Syn-C

Exhaust. Declare one damage type (Melee, Ranged, Arcane, or Entropy). Gain Immunity to that damage type until the start of your next turn.

> *"For a few seconds, one law of physics ceases to apply to you." — Daedalus Warning Label*
