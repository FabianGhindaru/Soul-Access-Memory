# Weapon Upgrades
Upgrades are installed into a weapon's available slots. Swapping upgrades between weapons is free outside combat. To install and use upgrades, you must have the corresponding Aptitude skill.

---

## Tier I

### Mantra Servo-Grip
**Type:** Both. **Activation:** Free (○).
**Price:** 300 Syn-C

Reduces the action cost to Draw or Swap to this weapon from your Inventory to Free (0 Beats).

> *"Magnetic holster interface. The weapon finds your hand before you reach for it." — Mantra Tactical Catalog*

---

### Daedalus Smart-Choke
**Type:** Both. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 400 Syn-C

Increases the weapon's Chaining Factor (CF) by +5.

> *"Optimizes pellet or energy spread." — Daedalus R&D Brief*

---

### Suppressor Shroud
**Type:** Ranged. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 300 Syn-C

Using this weapon while Hidden does not automatically break Stealth. If the target is completely unaware of you, they suffer −15 to their Dodge Defense Value (DDV) against this attack.

> *"They'll never see the muzzle flash." — Aftermarket Catalog*


---

### Counterweight Hilt
**Type:** Melee. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 300 Syn-C

When you are the target of a Melee attack, gain +5 DV for that defense. 

---

## Tier II

### Mantra C-Weave Feathering
**Type:** Both. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 600 Syn-C

While this weapon is drawn and active, increase your Max Movement pool by +5 tiles.

> *"Carbon-weave filament in the grip makes you move like the weapon isn't there." — Mantra Tactical Catalog*

---

### Twist-Rounds
**Type:** Ranged. **Activation:** Free (○)
**Price:** 600 Syn-C

Cadence. Your next attack with this weapon applies 🌀Twist on hit.

> *"Severe vertigo in each magazine."*

---

### Daedalus Overclocked Capacitor
**Type:** Both. **Activation:** Free (○)
**Price:** 700 Syn-C

Cadence. Take 15 Entropy damage to add +1 die to your Attack Roll this turn.

> *"Diverts power from safety limiters into the firing mechanism. — Daedalus Warning Label*

---

### Daedalus Grapple-Barrel
**Type:** Melee. **Activation:** 1 Beat (◐).
**Price:** 700 Syn-C

Fire a grappling line at a structural point within Medium Range. Pull yourself to that tile immediately. This movement does not cost from your Movement pool.

---

### Kinetic Siphon Matrix
**Type:** Both. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 600 Syn-C

Successful attacks with this weapon heal you for 2 HP.

> *"Violence as medicine."*

---

### Target Painter
**Type:** Ranged. **Activation:** 1 Beat (◐).
**Price:** 600 Syn-C

Mark one Being in Medium Range. The next attack against them by any ally gains +5 AV.

---

## Tier III

### Penetrator Slugs
**Type:** Ranged. **Activation:** Free (○).
**Price:** 1,000 Syn-C

Exhaust. Your next attack completely ignores the target's Mitigation Defense Value (MDV). They may still Dodge.

> *"Nothing between you and them."*

---

### Executioner's Edge
**Type:** Both. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 1,200 Syn-C

Gain +10 AV against any Enemy suffering from a Status Effect.

> *"The edge knows when its work is already half-done. It feeds on weakness."*

---

### Shatter Rounds
**Type:** Ranged. **Activation:** Passive.
**Price:** 1,000 Syn-C

Successful attacks with this weapon remove the **Resistant** keyword from the target for all damage types until the end of their next turn.

> *"Micro-resonance payload. Whatever they're proofed against — they're not anymore."*

---

### Sundering Strike
**Type:** Melee. **Activation:** Free (○).
**Price:** 1,000 Syn-C

Exhaust. Declare before rolling. The target's Armor Bonus is reduced by -10 for the rest of combat. Stacks once.
