## Archetype

**Hex Travel**
Reduce your Max Movement by 3 to increase your Voyager's Range by one step (e.g., Short to Medium), repeatable up to Very Long Range. This reduction reverses only while out of combat.

**Unfiltered Presence**
Once per session, walk into any restricted area, hostile standoff, or exclusive gathering exactly as you are. Instead of challenging you, everyone present will inexplicably grant you free passage. Performing harmful actions will stop this effect.

**Infinite Mirror**
The Eidolon begins with the Voyager, a unique Manifestation of themselves. The Voyager takes any reasonable shape desired, appears or disappears at will, and assists in both combat and narrative scenes.
In combat, besides the combat abilities, the Voyager can only attack an Enemy (1 Beat) in Short Range and deal arcane damage equal to double your current Level. They cannot be targeted and doesn't count as a Being.
Outside combat, the Voyager can be used to interact with objects or people. It has the same strength and voice as you.

**1. Oph (Free)**
Cadence. Command your Voyager to track an Ally in Short Range until the end of the next Player Phase, or when you decide to stop this ability. Push the Ally up to 8 tiles towards your tile for Free.
Ophelia: Push the Ally up to 8 tiles in any direction instead.

**2. Leo (1 Beat)**
Exhaust. Gain yourself or give immunity to an Ally in Short Range to all Ranged Attack Values below 20 until the start of the next Player Phase.
Leocadia: Set the immunity threshold to below 40 instead.

**3. Alt (1 Beat)**
Detach the Voyager to shield a Fading Ally in Short Range. The Voyager remains near the Ally and adds +10 bonus to Ally's Mitigation Defense Values. The effect ends when the Eidolon decides to end it (Free) or when the Ally is no longer Fading. Eidolon abilities cannot be used while this is active.
Althea: Add +20 bonus to Ally's Mitigation Defense Values instead.

**Special: //Ismene (Passive)**
Passive. Upgrade all Eidolon combat abilities to their true power. Alt becomes Althea. Iso becomes Isolde. Leo becomes Leocadia. Oph becomes Ophelia.
