### Dead In The Water (Tier V) 5HB ●●◐
**[ 4d8 (CF 12) Arcane ]**
Combative. Harmless. Manifest Despair Chains on an Enemy. While chained, the Being cannot move and takes Aeon Damage equal to your Soul Attribute. If the distance between the two of you is of Medium Range or more the Manifestation ends. The chained Being can try to win a Soul Clash at the start of each of their turns to escape. You must pay the Beat cost to keep this Manifestation up at the start of each of your turns.

### Pulverize Their Bones (Tier V) 5HB ●●◐
**[ Varies Melee / Ranged ]**
Cadence. Spend up to 20 current HP into your next Melee or Ranged attack. If the Enemy is defeated by this attack, convert excess damage back to your HP.
Amplify: Spend up to 25 current HP instead.

---
### Sanguine Contingency (Tier V) 5HB ●●◐
Exhaust. Combative. Swift. Swap HP with a willing Ally.
Amplify: No additional effect.

---

### It Came From Below (Tier V) 5HB ●●◐
**[ 4d8  (CF 12) Arcane ]**
Exhaust. Manifest an Eldritch Hand. The Hand fulfills one of your commands from this list then disappears:
Annihilate: Attack a Being in Long Range. This attack targets all other Beings adjacent to the initial Being.
Adjust: Instantly move to a chosen empty tile in Long Range.
Protect: Next Attack Sequence against you lower than 30 automatically misses.


---
### Death Toll (Tier V) 5HB ●●◐
Combative. Manifest a Waning Clock until the end of combat. While the Clock is manifested: successful hits apply 1 stack of Lethargy. Attacking with 3 or more stacks of Lethargy, destroys the Clock and applies Burst, Drain, and Twist to the targeted Being. Remove all Lethargy stacks. 
Amplify: Start with 1 additional stack of Lethargy.

---
### Touch of Madness (Tier V) 5HB ●●◐
**[ 3d10 (CF 12) Arcane ]**
Harmless. Influence a Being in Range. The Being must follow your command, but won’t do anything directly harmful or against their inner values.
In combat, you can choose to force the Being to move in a direction or not move at all, stop attacking you, etc. only if you win a Soul Clash.
_"The lunatic was inside my head all this time."_
